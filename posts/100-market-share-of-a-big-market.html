Microsoft has 100 percent rounded market share of the operating system of desktop and enterprise servers market. The same could be said for Google for the search market and Amazon for the bookselling market and online e-commerce market and even PayPal, eBay, Facebook, Twitter, Airbnb, WhatsApp, and so on depending on how we see things.

When someone has 1million followers on Twitter he or she has the 100% market of servicing the job to be done that caused those people if there are real persons who follow not bots. If the entrepreneur doesn't know what this market is then it is for sure credited in his account like a zero market share but if he or she learns to see what this market is by using the lens of the theory of disruptive innovation suggested by he or she could make a living with these1 million followers and more easily if he or she treats these people as respect-worthy people (everybody is) worth allocating time(everybody isn’t ) for knowing what matters to them and what interests them. The social media buzz thing is for sure a trap but for a serious entrepreneur or change maker in tech or in any kind of industry 1 million Twitter followers could be converted to a market serviced by a solution at 100% and if this entrepreneur or change maker learn and exploit the power of the network effect and its implementation then this tiny market can be very very very BIG.
