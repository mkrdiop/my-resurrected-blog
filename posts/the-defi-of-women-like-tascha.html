The DeFi of Women like Tascha

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Decentralization is a key concept that we should care about mainly when it’s about power. We are decentralizing power when we give girls and women more space to express themselves. From country management, politics to information systems, decentralization gives us opportunities to do more with less but decentralization more if it’s about systems requires collaboration and interoperability and these two pieces cannot go without connectivity. Connections are simply what actually fuel our everyday life and I hope this pandemic situation that we are trying to leave behind reminded us of the importance of being connected as human beings.

Financial services can be better when it’s decentralized that‘s in what Defiance believes and traditional finance can fear the coming solutions and innovation in this area of decentralized finance. The flash crash of May 16 and the recent stock squeezes of Gamestop and others that the financial markets experience make everybody think of the importance of coming up with a financial system more oriented toward people's pains and the real economy rather than profits made by a small set of high-frequency firms and large banks. My experience in the financial industry and my understanding of it let me think that their room for solutions like alpha finance to make a leap forward.
