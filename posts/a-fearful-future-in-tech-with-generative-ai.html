a fearful future in tech with generative AI

The future of Human-machine interaction is definitively in plain English

we all see how technology is shaping our life and work. it’s for sure scary to think about all these things that technology will do better than us, like writing a piece of text like this one that you are reading or developing an entire web application that works fine. Thinking about this may lead us to ask if there will be jobs left for us and if it will be possible to keep again this job in the years coming. Many questions about a doubtful future, a future of increasing chomage and low-pay jobs and the recent layoff heard on the other side of the Atlantic make me a bit more afraid about the future in tech and other industries because of this increasing productivity that artificial intelligence is providing to enterprises but one can be confident about the possibility to do something that only a human being can do and to live with it, also every time in past that we had such kind of fear of disruption more wealth and new jobs opportunities had been created. we can recall the many jobs and wealth the internet and the personal computer revolutions created when we were fearing that the automation of processing that came from to bring troubles in the job marketplace.

The first time I tested chatGPT I thought that there was no room anymore for one to be this software engineer because of the ability of AI to generate really well working and commented code but after some moments of thinking I ended up telling myself that this is a signal that calls us to level up our skills because as human beings we can do better than spending our time designing UML charts and writing code. Testing chatGPT showed me that time is over for technical workers to hide behind formal and domain-specific languages and time is for them to really learn how to better communicate and write really well the ins and out of the systems and apps that they are trying to build because no one will hire them for their only knowledge of specific languages and frameworks and to be sincere with the tech industry I should confess that testing generative AI gave me another opinion about all these layoffs that I have heard on podcast and news outlet like TechCrunch. This opinion is that tech companies are acting on a future improvement of the productivity of software engineers and developers that will easily increase by 100X fold in the coming years surely right in 2023.

For tech workers like you and me and even for other workers like journalists the path forward is to be specific about the work we do and chip by targeting this sweet spot that AI can’t disrupt while trying hard to exploit this same AI for increasing our productivity and perfecting our skills. If we stick to that I think the future could be less fearful with AI but this could require a complete reinvention of ourselves and our work and life.

In this fearful feature that I am describing real human connections will be a real lever, more than it actually is. Connecting and sharing with others, and building a support network of people with whom we could achieve things will help us overcome this danger of disruption of AI because it will take time for AI systems to become the social beings we are.

The future of Human-machine interaction is definitively in plain English so it might be possible to see in this future to see Alex and Natasha, Mary Anne, all journalists at TechCrunch crafting better systems and apps than Joel spolsky who is an excellent software engineer, and entrepreneur because journalists master that art of writing and communicating in plain English what will help them to better describe to this computer what they need to build for it to build it. This future seems far but when I was thinking about generative and AI and all the possibilities that could emerge from it I was not thinking that it would happen so soon. I was for sure minimizing the power of a group of connected people trying to tackle an issue and that by their efforts, the effect of scale, and the wisdom of the crowd they achieve many in a very small timeframe.

No need to be long, if we focus on a particular thing that computers cannot do and connect and share with others like social beings do AI will not disrupt us and this fearful future will be a brighter future.
