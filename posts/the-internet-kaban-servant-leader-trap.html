The internet Kaban Servant Leader Trap

Today’s internet-promoted influencers and tomorrow’s blockchained organizations

Diop Makhtar

Just now·4 min read

In the connected and wired world that we are living, I have been spotting a danger which is the growing size of interruption driven platforms that are built for exploiting your attention by using several methods that leverage the progress that we have made in cognitive science, sociology, and network effects but I have also spotted the subsequent week another danger which is the growing number of influential people who benefited from the democratization of communication tools to build tribes of people eager to do what these people are willing them to do. They are able to group people and make them work in a team-based setup often remotely (cost-efficient) for the goal of chipping something that may or may not be useful to the world or to these devoted persons but for sure benefic to the influential tribe leader. in such kinds of tribes often all members are qualified by the influencer as being a leader while there is clear evidence that all the members of a group cannot be all leaders of the group at the same time if decisions have to be made unless that there is a decision-making process involving the voice of each member with the same weighting. In such kind of setting Greenleaf notion of servant leader has in my opinion another meaning which is: serve me as I lead you or become my servant leader

The main audience of these influencers is people like you and me who are willing to take a personal path away from the industrial technocracy by using the power of the internet and the ease of access of information and communications that those influencers have used to make us believe that they are our leader because they provided us an important piece of information in our eyes at a given point in time where things were difficult to us and that we were looking for solutions. (1) Self and team entrepreneurs (often young), freelancers (young and employees not satisfied with their current jobs), key executives that failed to lead inside the organization they work and retired professionals that would make a second age experience with the things of the internet are those that are the type of profiles that these internet-promoted influencers are targetting.

Because they have grasped early the shift from a world where information is funneled and tunneled by big media firms to this open informational world driven by search engines fueled by the use of keywords and links and people’s behavior as a way of filtering and suggesting information, they became promoted by this web of computers and algorithms and connected to millions of people, some of them eagerly ready to work for free for them because of status but mainly because this behavior is what to expect given this kind of audience(see 1). This set of people often believe that they could leverage the voice of the influencer to raise their own voice, show their works, and get more awareness professionally. That’s professional networking which is often at the core of what I am describing here as a trap for those who are part of this audience of people that I have described.

As most of the elements of this audience are searching for an alternative path outside of the industrial system these internet-promoted leaders often show an anti-system and anti-industrialism and anti-capitalism image and mindset to align with the stories that these influenceable people are telling themselves. but is a flat organizational hierarchy prone to exponential growth and increasing productivity is a question worth asking.

The alternative is to do your own work with care and without following any kind of internet promoted leaders but I am not saying that you should avoid listening and reading them but you should acknowledge that you are the leader of this thing that you want to achieve and if it succeeds it will be mainly because of you or because of this new internet era of blockchained connections between people and organizations, blockchained connections that have clear terms and future outcomes defined right at the beginning and maybe adaptable int he futures given the elements inside of the initial terms.

Kaban is a great methodology for people and organizations that interests me like all these Toyota ways of managing people, processes, operations, and organizations because they all are great tools to could be improved but I think that this kind of blockchained organizations will need new management methodologies. I am working on this new set of methodologies and I think that you also should. But be careful with internet-promoted influencers.

PS: I am not pretending to lead anyone but rather to learn, build and chip interesting tech-enabled ideas to the world and I confess that up until there I have chipped anything of value other than these cheap words. But Thank you very much for reading me and please let me know if you want to teach me something.
