Right data, right places, right time

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Now a day if you want to be a linchpin of the financial industry, I mean a rockstar financial actor you should master Three keys fields. These fields are Financial engineering naturally, Sofware engineering obviously and telecommunication engineering apparently. You should also be curious about every kind of science that can help you leverage the power of analogy in order to apply discoveries and best practices of other fields into strategic investment insights or for solving interesting problems with different approaches than one who is a specialist could have because of his biased view caused by his excess of specialization that often blinds him from seeing things with the right cognitive glasses. To say that you should have open eyes to several concepts and things. This is more true if you aim to work in algorithmic trading or especially high-frequency trading for arbitrage and market-making where all these three fields play in sync and drive the markets across the world. For example here is a microwave antenna

Do you know that this antenna in order to be used in a financial network must be customized for speed because a financial system needs the right data, at the right place but mainly at the right time?

For customizing this antenna you should not only understand the data format used by exchanges and banks and all the specificities of the network system layers like TCP, UDP .. but you should be able to build a custom fast firmware for this antenna. It probably will not only help to be a rocket star C++ or C programmer or even one who could build a domain-specific language faster than the ones listed here.
