Gamer-centric Game Studios

Today all entrepreneurs are almost aware of customer experience and centricity. As game designers and developers we are creative entrepreneurs and the studios that we are running or working for are businesses and can be big tech companies like Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Google, and others. Gamers are for game studios what geeks are for Microsoft, nerds for apple, customers for amazon, and curious people for Google then like how these big tech companies are actually doing with respect to each “category” or t, games studios also should do everything with respect to making the gamers have the best happy experience by applying all the theories and best practices of the people-centricity revolution but maybe there are some industry-specific implementations of this concept of people-centricity methodology when it is about games products.

In this article, we are going to see how games studios and indie games developers could use this methodology or concept that I define as people-centricity, and in this specific case of the game industry, we could define this methodology or concept as gamer-centricity like how we could define it as customer-centricity if it was about the e-commerce industry. For sure a game studio could be seen as an eCommerce entrepreneurial journey and it is but let’s stick to our definition of gamer-centricity which is about chipping more fun game experiences that will make the gamer not only be funny and happy to play our games but would like and be eager to prescribe them to their networks of friends, families, and colleagues and all of them eager to know more about our actuals and coming games.

The ability of game studios (or indie game developers) to understand gamers’ situations, perceptions, and expectations.

The game industry has made huge progress in building sets of powerful gaming systems because we betted that the closer we were able to be to reality the more gamers will enjoy our gameplay experiences. This willingness to fit reality helped us figure out great technological stacks and methods of computation of games assets and interactivities. Thankfully the rise of mobile games helped us step back and think about this new way of seeing that is gamer-centric and you could see that there are many popular mobiles games that are actually only about playing with shapes and those games are really played by millions of gamers around the world. yes even if our studios are PC and/or console gamer-centric we should also take those who play mobile games as gamers and learn from them to make better PC and console games because the fundamental elements that make the player have fun could be the same but used and exploited in different ways.

For sure mobile games aren’t the same as video games because when we buy an Xbox it is not for playing games like candy crush to show that the medium that the targeted gamers will use to engage with your gameplay actually has an effect on the levers that you could use to design your game but that doesn't mean that simple shapes and mechanics couldn't be the bedground elements of your next PC and console video game if you decide to think enough about how a game emotionally impacts a gamer and provide value that will make it a shearable idea that every

The difficulty of often testing assumptions about how targeted gamers will engage with a gameplay experience because of the time of design and development

The game industry has hard constraints when it’s come to the application of the concept of customer centricity because this concept often involves testing the audience in order to know if the product (game experience in this case) assumptions that are made are relevant to this same audience because it takes a long time to design, develop and ship a small chunk of gameplay that could be tested by a set of gamers. This long period of getting testable elements unlike the web startups' world where a website or apps feature could be chipped in a matter of week is a very strong constraint that makes it difficult for games studios and indie game developers to fit their gameplays to the audience that they are seeking to serve in an agile way. Agility (in terms of gamer experience) is something very difficult to implement when producing games.

Those who play the game may not be those who buy it

Depending on the age of the gamers that you are targeting, the decision to opt for a game can be delegated because a game can interest a 10-year-old kid but if his parents find that the game is not aligned with their views and perspectives of the world and with how a game which their kids play should look like they will never let them adopt it. Game studios that are gamer-centric should care about this issue of delegated adoption for the games that there trying to design, develop and market what makes important the issue of knowing who the game is for and particularly their ages ranges.

The minimum viable audience of gamers because all gamers aren't the same

it is tempted to be willing to chip a game blockbuster played by millions of gamers but the new development of customer-centricity would like that we focus on a small set of customers even one only at a time and stick with this set of customers whom we could understand all their world views, situations, and perception in order to take them as an audience for which we will not build only on games but an audience that we will dedicate years of our creative work by always trying to chip them games that they will like. You can take this as segmenting the whole audience of gamers (there are actually ) given defined criteria that could be the type of games. For example, I am very sure that there is a set of gamers that actually only care about platformer games then if you have the gamer-centric mentality you could set your game studio as being a game studio designing, developing, and chipping only platformer games.

Another example of the exploitation of the possibility of customization of interactions with the kind of gamers that I could give you is this set of couples that are really passionate about gaming and who instead of consuming a Netflix movie comfortably and ‘’chaleureusely” would instead like to play together a game that is more likely to increase the level connectivity and interactivity of the relationship that they actually experience and this without obscenity (without landing to porn content).

Your game studio could focus on this audience of couples of gamers and make a decent business of chipping two-player games for couples and if unlike me you know enough about how sentimental relationships work psychologically and emotionally then you could even make other couples adopt your game because I really believe that a game could strengthen the link between two lovers but I may be wrong with this last statement about the possibility of games being the fuel of a stronger and passionate relationship between couples because I am now someone who know about this topic. these tweets that I was sharing in my tweeter profile were about this possibility that is an application of the methodology or concept of gamer-centricity which I am trying to expose in this article.

to be more specific you could say that there is this set of a couple of people of second age that care about gaming and set your game studio to target them. it is for sure not a huge set of gamers but as time goes on there will be more and more aged persons that care about video games and will have enough time to play them.

interaction capabilities and customization capabilities are two factors that will define how your customer experience, let’s say gamer experience, will be. Meaning a mass gamer experience, a targeted gamer experience, a niche gamer experience, and a one-to-one gamer experience. These are the four types of gamer experience that a game studio could choose to pursue as a strategy or which defined the actual strategy that may be unconsciously executed by it. however, Most game studios are by default pursuing a mass gamer experience because they have lowered the level of interactions and customization of the game that they design and develop an example of the weakening of the interactions possibilities between game studios and gamers is the abandoning of the support number that use to come with most games chipped during the early days of the gaming industry, a number that allowed the gamer to call when he or she was stuck into the game because of bugs or because of the difficulty of a level.

But now that each game studio has more interactions and customization capabilities thanks to the big progress we have made in terms of technologies and tools of communication and networking and the increased power of computation (both CPU and GPU), each game studio has the possibility of taking another more valuable kind of gamer experience that is not mass but targeted, niche or even a one to one gamer experience that would customize the gameplay in one gamer by one gamer basis.

The error of game studios like Hollywood movie studios is that they have chipped an amazing number of games and movie experiences without setting durable ways of interactions with those for who the produced game of the movie was mostly because all of them are set for themselves the goal of reaching more and more people because a successful game or film studio is the one that sells millions of films or games by making blockbusters not the ones with games played by 1000 and more gamers who can be loyal to the game and playing it year over year and without being reluctant to spend money for the game making it financially as successful as a hit game because if this long term customized relationships with gamers that has a high gamer lifetime value. Instead, game studios sacrificed this possibility of exploitation of the capabilities of interactions and customization that they have for the benefits of chipping hit games played by millions of gamers with star franchisable titles and characters. Some studios that we call now major like how they are called in Hollywood succeed at this strategy but this gamer mass strategy whereas all games titles and characters could never all become hit. Taking into consideration the lessons that we learn from the theory of the longtail could have helped us to know that this mass strategy is not for every game studio and it’s not the only path to getting and running a financially successful game studio.

all game studios could be both economically and socially successful with the new gamer revolution ignited by the increased capabilities of customization and interaction but it would need games studios to take a break and learn the ins and outs of the concept of gamer centricity and what they could do with this concept in long terms because the concept of gamer centricity is about longterm and maximization of gamer lifetime value, given the capabilities that they actually hold and the capabilities that they should acquire in order to implement and pursue a strategy that is based on this concept of gamer centricity.
