Godda Game, Four daily tasks to success

Godda Game is born from these four daily tasks

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Reading, writing, and coding while playing games during break times is a really good lifestyle that you can adopt. If you do it with care and professionalism it could be a very worth lifestyle that could economically provide you more than a living because each one of these activities can lead to paid gigs and contracts.

Reading will improve your understanding and cultural asset and ignite your writing experience.

Writing will structure your thoughts and flash out creative ideas. it will help you share your view with the world as I am doing right now. You have a lot of platforms for writing daily and with some of them like Medium, you could earn revenues.

Coding will give you this problem-solving skill that will make your writing more creative and your reading more educative while providing you the sense of achievement because one this function is up running and returning this result given that inputs provided you know that you have chipped something into the world and you could host it in GitHub so everyone could use it and know you are a skilly developer.

Hiding is just something that doesn't help you in this everyone's own media era that we are living in now. You should publish the works you do in these four activities for making your personal brand remarkable what can help you land your dream job and/or get better customers for gig contracts.

Being organized with a clear calendar that could help you develop habits will make you better at each of these four activities what in turn will drip by drip lead you to success.

Book-Dojo is about writing, reading, and coding, we don't still know where to put the playing part but Godda Games could be this game studio that will design, implement and publish all these creative gaming experience, platforms, and development tools that Book Dojo got from ideating around data-driven books and book-driven platforms because the world is not about job and knowledge only, Gaming can also be very useful to do from time to time.
