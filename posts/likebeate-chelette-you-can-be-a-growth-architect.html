Like Beate Chelette, you can be a growth architect

Diop Makhtar

Just now·3 min read

Mbenda is true when she advises you to not focus only on programming and software engineering because there are a lot of roles in the tech industry and all of them could be occupied by women. As an example consider the case of Beate Chelette who is a growth architect which is not a role easier than being a software engineer because growth is something that every company seeks to achieve and this is more true when it is about a tech company with an asset of tech-enabled products and services. In all ways, there aren't easy roles in the tech industry be this role in the front-office or in back-office, and this fact results from this adage that says that there isn't an easy job.

For the case of Beate Chelette, the charge is doubled because she is not only a Growth architect but also the founder of GrowthArchitect a strategy consulting agency that shares the same market with BGC. As if she was competing with these women who also are into tech assuming other roles
