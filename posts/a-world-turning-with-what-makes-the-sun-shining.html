I was focused on everything tech when the world was taking an inflection to make its underlying economy turns using nuclear power reactions because most countries like China, France, the UK, and others think that this path is the fastest way to get to fewer carbon emissions at horizon 2050. Like what this good resources about climate change says it’s not too late, not too late for me to get to know the state of this transition to nuclear power by most countries and by some heavy industries which are screening the many developments of big and small and modular nuclear reactors. To stay in everything big tech I am tempted to compare this transition to massive usage of nuclear power generation with the early days of personal computers when we transitioned from a small set of mainframe computer manufacturers to many manufacturers of OEM personal computers. For nuclear power generation, the same pattern is happening because many startups are trying to enter the market with small modular nuclear power plants of all kinds to compete with designers and operators of big nuclear power plants like Westinghouse, CNNC, and EDF. If we look closely, most sectors of our economy experience the same patterns of disruption at the same time or with a certain delay like how a crack in a reactor vessel flows through the width of the stainless steel that makes it but like with vessels we never hope these disruptions to lead to a catastrophic disaster.

In this past article, I highlighted how a pattern of disruption that happened in the stock market was being experienced by telecom operators of Africa which used to be the leaders of most fintech services like mobile payment.

Patterns of disruption

Participating in a hackathon organized by a telecom operator in my beloved country helped me spot what is a disruption…

medium.com

As a tech enthusiast, I am excited about the cleverness of nuclear power solutions and I am tempted to weigh out the dangerous safety problem of this kind of energy generation in the face of its beneficial usage like being able to produce as high as 1300MW (13,000,00 KW)of electricity while emitting as low as 2 to 228 gCO2/kWh what is very low compared to similar fuel and oil alternative and more efficient than eolian if take into account the land surfaced used to generate the same amount of energy.

In the preceding paragraph you could see that I have told so many good words in favor of nuclear power generation but let’s also keep in mind how many safety issues are tied to this kind of energy generation and Fukushima is here to remind us of this challenging goal of designing and operating safe nuclear power plant.

I think that it’s this same enthusiasm I show over nuclear power generation that led these many countries to adopt it but this should not prevent us from deploying more efforts for alternative power generation because maybe there is a solution that is way more efficient and safe than it. That’s why I think that the engagement of countries like Germany to not pursue nuclear power generation is of help for us because we would be sure that there is an entity here, not a small one, which will gonna scratch its head to find solutions as powerful as a nuclear power plant and seeing the last hydropower generation project that this country has completed make me more confident that german engineers may have not said their last words.

As a tech entrepreneur, any new market open to new entrants is an opportunity for me to ideate and contribute with ideas while trying to make something with these produced ideas then as you may have already guessed I switched from ReactJS to Nuclear Reactors because nuclear power generation is also about big technology like generative AI. In fact, I have already spotted a good idea at the intersection of these two trending big technologies, Generative AI and Nuclear power generation. I you follow me you could see that compounding things or breaking them into small parts, fusion or fission, have always been my way to come up with new kinds of entrepreneurial ideas. I am applying these same fusion and fission strategies in my quest for new modular nuclear facility concepts.

Like what every entrepreneur should do when entering a new space, I am learning the economics of nuclear power generation and recalling the few courses I had taken in nuclear physics crossing these with my mechanical engineering and software engineering skills to get new ideas while surveying the advances that have been made and to be frank with you I am seeing very interesting and promising nuclear power solutions from all the four corners of the world with some led by big key industrial actors like Rolls Royce and others by startups like Nuscale and maybe I will get to know soon your own contribution.

Then know that many entities are trying to fuel the world’s economic engine with fissile elements like uranium, plutonium, and other reactive elements with the goal of lowering our global level of emissions of CO2 with chain reactions and like these entities you also could be a nuclear power entrepreneur and contributes with your skills, ideas and nuclear concept and build if you think that nuclear power generation is a good solution for transitioning, maybe temporary, in a less carbonated world. if you do it while helping people with cancer your contribution would be a very impactful one. I am trying, and I hope you also will.
