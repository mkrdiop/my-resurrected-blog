A New Symphony of Art is About to Begin

Why I’m convinced AI will unleash more hits than ever before — and the story of Zikcut.

Diop Papa Makhtar
·
May 29, 2025

--

Just think about it for a second. A 60-second beat, paired with a simple, engaging video of the same length, and BAM! You could potentially reach millions of viewers in a single day. Let that sink in. The Beatles, those legendary pioneers who shaped so much of modern music, couldn’t have even conceived of that kind of instantaneous global reach for their art. Imagine trying to explain to John, Paul, George, and Ringo the sheer velocity at which a piece of music and its visual counterpart can now travel across the planet, connecting with audiences on an unprecedented scale. It’s mind-boggling.

And while it feels almost certain that no single viral song today could ever replicate the cultural phenomenon and enduring legacy of The Beatles, something even more profound is brewing on the horizon. What truly captivates me, what has me practically buzzing with excitement, is the astonishing rate of progress we’re witnessing in generative AI models. This isn’t just a minor technological tweak; it feels like a seismic shift in the very landscape of art creation.

Think about it. These generative models are rapidly becoming incredibly sophisticated, capable of crafting music and visuals with a level of creativity and nuance that was once solely the domain of human artists. And the implications of this are staggering. I genuinely believe that these AI tools will, in the not-so-distant future, unleash a torrent of hit songs and captivating music videos onto the market, potentially dwarfing the entire historical output of the traditional music industry — and doing so in a drastically shorter timeframe.

It’s this very realization, this profound understanding of the transformative power of AI in the creative realm, that has pulled me back into the media industry. It’s like unearthing a treasure I’d almost forgotten. Years ago, I had a video-on-demand platform that ended up being shelved. But the core idea, the desire to connect content with audiences, never truly faded. And now, seeing the exponential growth of generative AI, I felt an undeniable pull to revisit that ambition, this time focusing squarely on the music segment.

This brings me to what I’ve been pouring my energy into lately: https://www.zikcut.com/. With Zikcut, I’m making a bold bet. I believe that a significant majority of the songs and music videos we’ll be consuming in the coming years will be generated, at least in part, by artificial intelligence. And here’s the kicker: I also believe that consumers won’t particularly care. The magic will be in the experience, the emotional connection with the music and the visuals, regardless of whether a human or an algorithm was the primary creator.

This opens up a fascinating and somewhat surreal possibility: the emergence of music stars who are essentially ever-living artificial characters. Imagine artists who can perpetually evolve, whose music and image can seamlessly adapt to changing tastes, who exist purely in the digital realm. It’s a concept that blurs the lines between creator and creation, between the organic and the synthetic.

Of course, this isn’t to say that human creativity will become obsolete. Far from it. I envision a future where human artists collaborate with AI, using these powerful tools to amplify their own visions, to explore new sonic and visual territories, and to push the boundaries of what’s possible. It’s a partnership, a synergy between human ingenuity and artificial intelligence, that I believe will lead to an explosion of artistic innovation.

Zikcut, in my mind, is positioned to be right at the heart of this evolution. It’s a platform built for this new era, a space where AI-generated music and videos can find their audience, where these digital artists can connect with listeners and viewers. It’s about embracing the future of music, not fearing it. It’s about recognizing the immense potential that lies within these technological advancements and creating a space where that potential can be fully realized.

This journey back into the media landscape, driven by the incredible advancements in AI, feels like coming full circle, but with a completely new set of tools and possibilities. I’m genuinely excited about what the future holds for music and for platforms like Zikcut. It’s a wild ride, and I’m thrilled to be on it.
