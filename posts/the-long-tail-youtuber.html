The long tail YouTuber

Most people won't miss something new. if it is about video content publishing you better have to do such that every time a new viewer comes to your channel she finds new things to see. By doing this you will make your audience always think that maybe there is a new thing that’s going on on your channel. You can choose to have branded content by trying to imitate large media tv but you better have to use a long tail strategy by just publishing everything you can record.

Of course, it will help to hire or be a good software engineer for building this software that will brand in mass your content using an algorithmic video production pipeline.

I have tested a long-tail video publishing strategy with a massive video content generation script. 8 months until this test I see that this long tail strategy works but it just needs clear insight about keywords and subscribers if it is youtube the target medium. Getting subscribers is the hardest part of this. I won’t buy them. How can I get them?
