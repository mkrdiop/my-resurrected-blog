Curiosity gap and search engines or startups

Diop Makhtar

13 hours ago·1 min read

You probably have already read all the short form articles I made about searches that are triggered by the consumption of other media like news, events, talkshow, TV, book or blog. This behaviour is triggered by what cognitive science calls the curiosity gap. A lot of the searches we do in search engines are the results of this effect. It helps to know this effect of our brain asking to get more information for designing a better search engine or for making better search engine marketing. I even have a proved tactic that exploits this and makes people search with the keyword I want. With such thing in hand, it is not easy to make the choice of doing things that matter rather than just exploiting it to sell dump products and services. A simple question can activate the curiosity gap and make us look for the answer. Asking question is for sure a great marketing tool not only for knowing your audience but for getting attention too.

Curiosity gap driven traction is what I am tempted to call this kind of traction.
