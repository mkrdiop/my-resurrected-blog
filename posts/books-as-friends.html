Books as Friends

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Friends are part of what could help someone be level up because they can provide insights, advice, motive, and care. Through discussions, friends highlight us what matters most and the set of possibilities for us to achieve what we seek to achieve. Friends are teachers, mentors, advisors, collaborators, employees. They are part of our social connections and extend our senses being where we cannot be and representing us where they are. What I was missing is that friends can be something else than real human beings. Friends can be this pet and better for me they can be this book that you are reading. Books are real opportunities for friendships and like our real friends they can represent us anywhere in anytime in the past and the future. Then Authors of Book have an important place for those who take books as friends and all other persons that helped this author come up with this book have this same important place.

I was highlighting the fact that technology has not still yet helped us make books become a medium for connections what they are whatever their formats are. Today A quick connection with some people that I thought being friends made reminded me that often books are better friends than human beings then my thoughts go to all these authors I have read because they all are now my friends and hope you also will.

real and impactful friendship through books can be a very impactful solution for our world. Religions have already us this statement even if we ended making religions a personal thing that would not manage our society. The most powerful religions are all driven by books. We can create such a kind of thing again with the help of our advances in technology and make it be relevant and helpful for promoting justice, freedom, wealth, diversity, and equality. Yes, books have the power of allowing us to achieve all of these but we are abandoning them in favor of some tweets online and short discussions with people who don't care about us while a book that we are reading always cares about us.

Books as Family could unbelievably have more impact than our real family that does not mean that family is not important. I may be wrong with all of these thoughts but I am sure that books are very important for our society.
