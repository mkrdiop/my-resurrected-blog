Animes Search Engine for Mbenda

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Mbenda N’dour is a software engineer but she also likes animes. She does vlog about software engineering and about self-care and her journey as miss California.

Mbenda edits carefully the videos that she publishes on youtube. She often illustrates them with scenes coming from her favorite anime and the scene dialogs always fit the topic of her vlog. By doing this Mbenda is naturally promoting those animes that she likes and converting people like me to go and see those anime but the amines creators and publishers don't ease this job for Mbenda because she has to remember a word or a specific action that she has already seen in one anime and go cut this piece to put it into the vlog she is editing what is time-consuming and require a good memory also.

Here the solution is that the anime creators and publisher should make their animes video content searchable with keywords like google do for online content. With such a solution, Mbenda could just go to this anime search engine and search for specific keywords or sentences. More, this anime search engine could also, once the keywords or the sentence are found, allow to cut easily the interesting part of the anime in order to put it where a user like Mbenda want to put it.

This is applicable to all video content like Hollywood movies and even youtube videos and this a marketing tool for those movies producers because it eases the sharing of their movies and makes them more spreadable online. Remember that the thing that spread the most is always more successful online.

It was a long time ago that I had this idea but Mbenda reminded me of it while I was seeing her vlog. It’s an amazing tool that you can build to make easy the life of creative video producers like Mbenda.

This idea will very likely be part of one of the use cases of this upcoming data-driven book title Engineering as a Marketing tool that Book Dojo will publish soon but meantime if you can implement it, please do that because people like Mbenda can’t wait to use it and I am very occupied by other interesting ideas (Games, Books, and Fintech software).
