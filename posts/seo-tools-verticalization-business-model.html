Seo Tools Verticalization business model

Diop Makhtar

Just now·1 min read

Seo Tools and services are there waiting for you to take and verticalize them by industry because each industry has its own SEO needs. Do you think that a lawyer and civil engineer have the same SEO needs and ways to use SEO? You maybe have already seen how words define who we are in my last article about how words help us understand our brains and in the works of johnson and Lakoff up until Kahneman. This is why an SEO tool should be specific to a given industry for a given audience. It is not about collecting keywords and statistics but about understanding how we use words to express our needs and vision, how they describe the actions we seek to perform, and the goals we want to achieve.

of course, all of this is about words and numbers then Mathematics, Cognitive science, and psychology matter a lot for it to be a better SEO tool and for marketing it better. Once the knowledge about these fields is mastered, Computer science is there to help you go faster and scale.

Words and Numbers games are not dead you have a lot of smart things to do with these kinds of games and blogging.
