The Whispers of Everyday Objects

Finding Meaning in the Mundane

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
·
Feb 8, 2025

--

In my opinion, everything around us speaks to us. They try to give us subtle indications, guiding us towards the right path. If we pay close attention to what happens around us and what enters our lives, and make an effort to interpret these occurrences, we begin to understand the "language of things."

A prime example can be found in Africa, specifically in Senegal. When you purchase something, the seller often wraps it in pieces of paper. These are usually remnants of unsold newspapers, imported from other countries, finding a new lease on life in Senegal. They serve a practical purpose – packaging goods.

A few years ago, I began a playful experiment. I took pictures of these discarded newspaper pieces and posted them on Twitter, tagging the original newspaper's Twitter account. This was a way of demonstrating how these pieces of paper, thousands of miles from their place of origin, were reaching the end of their lifecycle.

However, as I mentioned earlier, everything around us speaks to us. These seemingly insignificant pieces of paper had a story to tell. Lately, every time I've bought something, the packaging has included either a piece of paper featuring comics or one with product advertisements. The message from these papers is clear: "Don't forget your dream of producing animated films."

This idea has been simmering in my mind for a long time, and these seemingly random occurrences serve as a gentle reminder.

I hope this illustrates how even from the most mundane and seemingly insignificant aspects of our lives, like the packaging of a purchased item, we can glean valuable insights and reminders about our own personal journeys and aspirations.

Expanding on the Core Idea:

This incident with the newspaper packaging sparked a deeper reflection within me. It led me to question: How often do we overlook the subtle messages that surround us? We are constantly bombarded with information, yet we often fail to truly see, hear, and understand the deeper meanings embedded in our everyday experiences.

Think about the objects that surround you at this very moment. Your phone, your laptop, the clothes you are wearing, the food you ate for breakfast – each of these items has a story, a journey, and a purpose. By paying closer attention to these seemingly insignificant details, we can begin to unravel a deeper understanding of ourselves and our place in the world.

For example, the worn-out shoes in your closet might remind you of a memorable trip, a challenging hike, or a period of intense growth. The chipped mug on your desk might evoke memories of a cherished friendship or a comforting ritual. These objects, seemingly insignificant, become portals to the past, reflections of our present, and perhaps even hints of our future.

The Art of Observation:

Cultivating the art of observation is crucial in this endeavor. It requires us to slow down, to become more mindful of our surroundings, and to question the "why" behind the things we encounter. Why is this particular color dominating my social media feed? Why am I drawn to certain types of music at this point in my life? Why do I keep seeing the same number sequence everywhere I look?

These seemingly random occurrences may not always have profound or significant meanings. However, the act of questioning, of seeking deeper connections, can be incredibly rewarding. It can lead to unexpected insights, spark creativity, and foster a deeper sense of awareness and appreciation for the world around us.

Beyond the Material:

The "language of things" extends beyond the material world. The conversations we have, the books we read, the movies we watch, the music we listen to – all these experiences leave an imprint on our souls. They shape our perspectives, influence our decisions, and contribute to the tapestry of our lives.

By paying attention to the messages conveyed through these experiences, we can gain a better understanding of our values, our passions, and our purpose. Perhaps a particular song resonates with us deeply because it reflects a hidden longing within our hearts. Maybe a conversation with a stranger sparks an unexpected realization or opens our minds to a new perspective.

Embracing the Journey:

The journey of deciphering the "language of things" is an ongoing one. It requires patience, curiosity, and a willingness to embrace the unexpected. There will be times when the messages are clear and profound, and other times when they remain elusive and enigmatic.

The important thing is to remain open to the possibilities, to trust your intuition, and to enjoy the process of discovery. By paying closer attention to the world around us, we begin to see the interconnectedness of all things, and we begin to understand that even the smallest details can hold profound meaning and significance.

The world is a tapestry woven with countless threads, each one telling a unique story. By learning to read the "language of things," we begin to unravel the intricate patterns and discover the deeper meaning of our existence. This journey of self-discovery, of connecting with the world around us, is a lifelong adventure, filled with wonder, surprise, and a profound sense of awe.
