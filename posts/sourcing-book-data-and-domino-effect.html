Sourcing book data and domino effect

often, Reading one book led to willing to read another one. To say that there is a domino effect from book to book that may be a computer could find with book sales data and book reading platforms data. This is already pinned by Seth Godin in his article backlist and frontlist on the domino project blog. This is a book sales predictability feature and a way to measure a kind of domino effect about books but outsourcing this kind of data is not an easy task. It is easier for companies like Amazon or penguin or shelter to do that given they have time and computational resources to go deeper into the content of their book collections and sales data but it is not something that would keep you give a try as I did with Book Cube.

BookCube is on its way.
