Books and searches links

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
Dec 4, 2020

--

If you know what books someone read and the history of all websites she visited you could get the correlation between her book reading experience and online experience. This is a valuable piece of information that can be sourced and built by an independent data aggregation team or company. Books drive search as I said in one of my last article news drive searches. Books like news drive search so knowing the relationship between book and search is a good perspective feature or a signal that could tell us how to improve books or search for the benefit of people. Book reader's requirements about search online are not the same as usual internet users.
