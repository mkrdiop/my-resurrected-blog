Third-Party & Real-time

Third-party is about connecting two or more different things for them to cooperate for producing better things. Simply said, Real-time is right now without delay and without complexity at all. These two pieces of technology, coupled with artificial intelligence, are the future of innovative products and services, said short, the future of this world. All coming digital services and products should leverage these technologies for delivering better user experience and value. That’s why they interest us.
