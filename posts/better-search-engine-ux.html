Better search engine UX

Diop Makhtar

Just now·1 min read

Yes, google’s John Mueller copied content doesn't benefit the users like us, and better than avoiding content duplication a well-designed search engine must also care about content continuity. This content continuity is achievable now since we have all the users' behavioral patterns data, the natural language processing tools, and the artificial intelligence algorithms to do it.

suggest 10 pages that will provide continuous content or information given the user's predictable visit behavior is something possible for the to date technology.

When the user leaves the first webpage of the results he or she consumed the second page that will be consumed must complete what she or he probably consumed with the first and As already stated alternative point of view will help the user fight against the confirmatory bubble.

Google, Bing, Duckduckgo, and other search engines are doing good it just happens that we have the possibility to make things better so let’s make them better.
