A new kind of Web Creator Economy

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
3 min read
·
1 hour ago

Writing every day may be enough as a contribution to what we all share that is the world. at least my hands get something to do that may be less valuable than these things you have been getting your hands dirty on. it’s hard to sustain the habit of doing things that have no financial results attached to them if the performer doesn't manage to get his physiological needs satisfied by means of a paid job. I have been thinking that we have failed to build around the web an economy that could support the basic needs of sharers this is what the creator economy should all be about that is creating an economic system that would allow anyone who contributes with physical and digital elements to get some part of these billions of dollars flowing around the web no matter how many people engage with what he has contributed but instead of that we have developed creators platform that values audience because the business model dynamics of these creators platform could only survive if the number of users both creators and consumers continue to increase with a focus on consumers because it’s easier to get some folks to create things than to get people to buy the created assets.

Looks as if I was plaiding for my own case by crying my desperateness to get my basic needs satisfied by the only fact of writing and sharing online but this goes further than that because what I am willing to highlight is the opportunity of building a new web economy where there is a financial outcome attached to any kind of particular actions performed by users, both consumers and creators. When blockchains technologies showed up, I was a bit more confident that this kind of incentivized web would soon be built on top of one of these blockchain technologies but almost all these blockchain financial solutions ended up imitating what has been the strength of conventional financial markets that is offering people and organizations a way to get profit from the fluctuations of assets and for the case of blockchain-based assets like bitcoin the amplitude of fluctuations is so high that they ended looking like gambling frontend than serious financial systems but one could easily see that conventional financial firms ended adopting these highly volatile crypto assets because for these conventional financial firms which were getting a hard time any funnel of profits was good to take.

The internet is now made of billions of users then how to design and build such a creative web platform where all actions are financially incentivized is for sure a very hard question to answer. I first thought that chatGPT could help find some reasonable answer and here is what says ChatGPT

I myself have not read the whole answer but it looks like the user reward system section would be the most important part of this all users, both creators, and consumers, incentivized web platform but from where will come the money to give to users? Perhaps any participant could contribute with a given capital in order to be part of the system and like a stock this participation would grow according to the value of the stock of the platform and to the actions performed by the users. for the user participation part substack, the creator platform for writers has recently opened its capital to users as reported by TechCrunch on … in this article entitled …..

then my question could be turned into how to design and build a platform like substack with which both writers and readers are financially incentivized.

I am thinking about possible answers to this question. Once I get some answers maybe I will write about them here. if you have some ideas let me know but meanwhile thank you for reading. Go make your contribution to the world whatever the form of this contribution is. mine is actually these words you have been reading I hope I will be able to do more soon but I am happy that I can at least do this little.
