Curiosity, Subconsciousness, and analogy

Diop Makhtar

·Dec 23, 2020

If self-preservation and optimization of biological resources is part of our base animal brain function, it seems obvious that our neural system uses analogy as a preservation and optimization tool by trying to fit past experiences and stimulus to the new we are experiencing. It sounds like it is the core level of differentiation between us and the animals’ kingdom because we do have way more ability to achieve this than our animal peers. Given this statement experiencing a lot and navigating from domain to domain to domain is the best path for finding new efficient and smart things than focusing on one thing only.

Be curious so have more value than stay focused unless your focus is about curiosity.
