Elsewhere data for Data science and machine learning

As Datascientist you are like this genetical engineering expert working with chromosomal materials by Composing, sequencing, coding, decoding, decoupling, mutating, assembling, and interpreting. Like the genetical element that the genetical engineer has as base elements for analysis are often incomplete. This is more true for those who work in the study of archeological biology trying to break the genetic code of species like dinosaurs because they got disrupted by the effect of the time past. One piece of genetical material in this species found here in Patagonia and this other same species found here in Africa, another one found elsewhere piece by piece the genetical engineer with his knowledge, skills and the huge progress we made in technology try to end up with a complete sequence of the ADN of the species of his or her focus. In the molecular world, there are species that share genetical Patrimoine with the species that the genetic engineer studies. This is a form of cloning applied if there are not enough genetic material segments available for the species the genetic engineer is focused on.

The expert data scientist face often the same situation in front of which she or he have not significant real data about the topic of its analysis and then uses his skill and creativity for finding other real data that can be similar quantitatively and qualitatively to the missing real piece of data that make his of here training dataset enough populated to produce meaningful and statistically relevant results. it is common sense for data scientists to be involved in genetic engineering and evolution theory because the data they study are like ADN they can change in time mutate, translate …

the theory of probability and the theory of evolution both matter for these two fields that can be seen as disjoint if we don't wear the correct cognitive glasses that help us see that they simply are the same.

A data scientist in front of historical data with missing elements applied this genetic engineering concept and built interesting models that were not buildable with the only data that were there. Creative and innovative data that can extend datasets of real measurements of which we have not enough data for applying machine learning always mattered.

Glad that I knew that it mattered and I hope you too.
