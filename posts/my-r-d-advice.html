My R&D advice

Diop Makhtar

22 hours ago·1 min read

I highly advise you to often see what matters for other sectors and try to find other products and services they can build using their actual resources and materials to say exploiting their current industrial processes and technology stacks. A lot of companies are so focused on their core profitable activity and given the strength and pressure of difficult economies, they under-invest time and money in research and development.

You curious scientist is the only one who can see what they cannot see because you travel from idea to idea and know-how analogy works. You are the one who can transfer knowledge gathered from other fields, sectors, and categories to another field, sector, and category because you know how ideas spread. You studied where innovation comes from and saw that it never comes from focusing on a core field and having deep expertise in one only thing but by exploration, navigation, and experimentation of different ones.
