Advantage of a math-ruled business model

The example of Google and the internet-era business models

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

A search engine is based on mathematical models and deals with numbers and words. By the way, it's possible to always know the number of resources needs to keep it running. This operating cost is a function of the mathematical model used and there is a very very very small likelihood to see upfronts operating cost. In front of such kind of business model, most attention that is allocated to managing, hedging, and improving operating costs in normal businesses goes to making the model itself better and useful to its audience.

Most internet-era business models are like that. This is what I am tempted to call the business model of the internet era. This kind of business model is made possible by the advanced progress we have made in computing words and numbers with electronic devices and the personal computer revolution made it accessible to every one of us given that a smartphone is also a personal computer.

Businesses that lies on computing numbers and words are computable businesses. They almost always are manageable and understandable with mathematical formulas. If you manage yourself have formulas that can make them better and useful. You will have a business that I cannot name actually.

Google is a computable business, google used to care about math and mathematicians, I don't know if they still care about it, I don't know if Google has formulas to make itself better. What I do know is that I have formulas to make myself better, I care about math and I am a mathematician running Book Dojo a mail-only startup publishing data-driven books, building book-driven platforms for book communities and book lovers.
