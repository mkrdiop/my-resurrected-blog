From classifiers to clarifiers

I searched for the button write before I started to write this article and this is certainly due to the fact that it has been a long time since I have not written and published something here on my medium blog. This happened certainly because these past days, I was more engaged in a project involving my very close local environment which is this pretty city that is Dakar with its range of mysteries and treasuries one of which I got the chance to learn by visiting one of its amazing places which is close to me and my very insightful environment that is this room as office in my mother’s home.

Engineers often need to leave their comfortable working zone in order to get insights about interesting engineering processes and ideas to test and validate. Most of today’s innovative engineering solutions were got by their inventors and implementors when they were doing something unexpected that often is very far from their core work and field of interest.

I have made several engineering mistakes in my career but I am glad that I have learned many interesting things turning around streets in Paris when I had any job to be done. Things like this peaceful community living apart from others both in their way of looking and the simple and nice architecture of their homes all filled with gardens on each of their right and left sides. A community that is like these isolated communities of the USA that I learned by reading and being interested in American culture. Culture is involved in what I have learned visiting this place of Dakar because I learned an interesting thing about agriculture, particularly a key piece of knowledge about the medicine of plants, trees, and probably many other kinds of vegetal species

During this out my room as office time far from my comfort zone, I have also seen how places can hold treasuries hidden and ignored by their most close inhabitants certainly because most of us think that oil, gas, and metals are the most profitable underground resources when there are more interesting resources surfacing by themselves from underground to the ground with their utilities unknown to us and worse often taken as disturbing elements, as something to hide and throw somewhere where it will not bother someone anymore, somewhere like the Atlantic Ocean a place that Dakar share with other cities like New york.

Some day ago one of these kinds of treasuries landed on my feet and I learned from it but my closeness to it badly inspired me to not take the time to take some samples of it. This is the effect of closeness, most of the time it makes many insights become unnoticed by us. My closeness to this place made me think that it will not be difficult to pick some samples of this treasury in order to get some hard chemical and physical properties of it but I was wrong to think like that because I am not sure of what tomorrow will be, things could change so promptly making things that were close to us become so far away. Biais is a very dangerous flaw for experts and engineers who could end up being in a bubble due to their over-specialization and expertise in one particular topic or field. One of these mistakes I made in my career is ignoring mechanical engineering and manufacturing after I decided to follow the path of systems and software engineering which was more interesting to me. Writing about this mistake I remembered this agricultural lab that was close to our dorm rooms when I was a student in mechanical engineering. During those times I was not aware that agricultural biology was a thing but not that almost everybody now that we are heading to a food and water crisis due to climate change knowledge about agriculture biology is ever more important because it could help us improve the productivity of agricultural fields. What I have discovered these past few days looks like a solution to this water and food stress we are heading to but again I am crying for victory before taking the weapons and going to war, I am selling the skin of the animal before sacrificing it because I actually have any hard factual data about this solution that I am suspecting to work. What I am sure about is that randomly leaving a comfort zone is very beneficial to entrepreneurs because it helps get new ideas and insights then if you have time go visit a place near you and try to forget a bit about what you are interested in and the problems you are trying to solve because if you do you may end up with interesting ideas, insights and probably a solution to this problem that you are trying to solve but if your cross a plant, a tree or any other vegetal species pay attention and don't arm if you can take care please do
