The successful start of Youtube Shorts

Like if we were dating with short videos as we are trying to date with Tinder

Diop Makhtar

Just now·4 min read

Short video content was an obvious category like how animated gifts were in the early days of the internet and still today. The Internet seems to be mostly about memes and most of them are memes of the past that we reshape and experiment with the technology of today. Short video content that delight the audience in less than a minute was already a form of content experimented by advertising agencies for introducing brands of products and services to us consumers of media and this form of content became itself a whole new category of content that can be used for delivering not only promotional information but for informing, teaching and delighting people.

TikTok exploited this new form of short video content with many videos that are mainly about entertaining young consumers of the generation Z with shorts movies that make them laugh, fragmenting, by the way, the audience of this mediatic mastodon that is is youtube but like how each big tech giants does when a strong thread comes from an incumbent Youtube reacted with a subproduct called youtube short like how Twitter reacted to clubhouse with Twitter spaces.

Yesterday night I have been testing this new feature of youtube in order to taste the kind of videos that are already offered and unlike what I think TikTok is about Youtube shorts is made of more serious informational and motivational shorts video and the theories that I have concerning short video content and long video content seems to hold because of my own testing experience. This theory that I am talking about is this assumption that we are more likely to watch five successive short one-minute videos than an entire one that is 5 minutes long and I am almost sure that youtube’s team will test this assumption that I am exposing and discovering that better it will be for them to suggest you these 5 short videos than this 5 minute one that I have produced.

Shorts videos will compete with many videos that are less than 5 minutes long and the level of creativity that I have seen during my yesterday nightly test tells me that many videos with a length between one to five minutes will experience audience outage in front of their alternative shorts version that will be favored by youtube which is actually promoting the adoption of this new competitive feature up until offering a $100M fund for supporting with revenues the most successful shorts videos creators (here).

The big tech competitive landscape is made of moves of new entrants that make big established startups react with the introduction of new features that emulate the solution of the new entrants or use a positioning strategy that can help this big tech startup prevent the churn of a part of their big audience. That’s what Youtube has done with Youtube shorts as a reaction to the thread of TikTok. But like many new features, there are always things that could be optimized and made better and in terms of design, my take is that Youtube could also exploit the Dating app meme with applications like Tinder by allowing Youtube short users to use the swipe left and right as a way of liking or not liking the actual short on the screen like if we were dating with short videos as we are trying to date with Tinder.

The battle between tech startups and incumbents is an evergoing battle and each day teams of engineers, marketers, and designers are working hard to bring this new idea that will provide the company that they are working on this leap that will help them have a competitive advantage in front of others. That’s what happens in every industry but in the big tech industry, this effect is not only amplified bu it is also accelerated.
