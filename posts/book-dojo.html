Book Dojo

A dojo is a place for learning, collaborating a place for expressing art. A place to be a master in something, the thing you like.

It is a place of discipline in the actions you should take to achieve your goal.

Here the goal is to publish a successful book for sharing this idea you have using a book as container and all possible media to make it spread.
