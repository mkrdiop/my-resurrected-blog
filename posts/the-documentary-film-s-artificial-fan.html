The Documentary Film’s Artificial Fan

The dots representing locations on this white map on this documentary I watched for a few seconds inspired me this documentary Artificial Fan (DAF) that I am writing about after writing about this yellow sticker that is about JS dependencies switching and swapping. Who knows? Maybe these two topics are related because all I will be writing about in this story is to some extent also about computational journalism because it’s about producing web content and fan engagement experiences and platforms as well as automated conversations in social media and all of these autonomously.

if you have some knowledge in web development and I am almost sure that you have it because most of those who read me are people who know more about programming than I do but I don't know why I am telling you the importance of the role of javascript for web development for showing you that it may be linked to the topic of this story that is also a computational journalism exercise because you can see what I will describe and develop here will be intended for the web and could require javascript and its range of modules, tools and frameworks all of them as useful as this couple of programming languages that is C/C++.

The identification of the materials used in a documentary, materials like archives, interviewed people, showcased facts like documents, and the gathering of more information about these materials for the purpose of creating even more content about them and crediting the people and organizations behind these materials is the purpose of this documentary Fan bot that I am about to describe and implement. Then I could say that this documentary fan bot is about producing engaging content autonomously for the fanbase of the underlying film documentary that it covers for the goals of having more reliable sources and materials about this film documentary first streamed on video streaming platforms like Netflix, Discovery, ….

This Documentary Fan bot that I am trying to describe in this story is about how one could use a computer to take any produced documentary Like … and produces autonomously rich web content and social media discourses from this film documentary with what we call artificial intelligence and its special custom field that is machine learning.

This AI web production pipeline will use all these smart technologies like voice recognition, facial recognition, Voice to text, natural language processing, OCR, and others that compound to form the building block of today’s AI stack for producing creative content that will make more people and fans engage into the conversation about the covered film documentary and consume complementary materials about the topic covered by this documentary that they have watched. This documentary fan bot is about igniting and continuing the discussions about any document that has been prime-timed on any video streaming platform.

While writing this last paragraph, I thought that a social media platform specially designed for conversations about documentaries is something that people may want and need like these social media platforms specially built for conversations about books, Platforms like Goodreads or Shelfari that was acquired by amazon books.

We could imagine that this Artificial Fan intelligence that I’m actually writing about could feed this documentary-based social network with creative social media materials that will ignite the discussion of real fans of documentaries in a way that will promote these documentaries.

This artificial fan intelligence is all about driving the conversation about these documentaries by collecting, producing, and publishing additional facts and web content about the topic covered.

I am a fan of documentaries of all kinds, particularly documentaries about science and nature like those that are often streamed on channels like planet, discovery, curiosity stream, Netflix, HBO, and others but my mind tells me that this Documentary Artificial Fan if it’s implemented as I see it will help every fan discover more information about the topics covered by the documentaries they love and engage in conversations with other fans about such topics.

This documentary Artificial fan (DAF) will help fans like me find more information, facts, and materials about the topics covered by the last documentary we loved watching. The other advantage of this DAF is that it is a knowledge gatherer and classifier that helps have a clear view in this noisy and crowdy information era which requires more and more smart information filtering systems that are smart enough to know what kind of information we need and are able to find this piece of information autonomously helping us save time browsing around the internet in quest of meaningful information. It’s not like a search engine it’s more than that even if the advantage of this DAF that I am highlighting was the idea that search engines sold us at the beginning of the internet but it’s easy to see now that almost all search engines are actually more oriented toward revenues maximization achieved by improving ad system more than they are oriented on helping us find information that interest us because we (at least me) are persons of interest. If we were using a film series like Person of interest as the input of this DAF the result would be the same and the system would work like how I described it in the subsequent paragraphs.

As I am always seeking to give practical examples even if I often fail to achieve this, I will be using one documentary as material and try to develop a minimal version of this DAF that I have described for you to see but most importantly is this documentary fan social media is a platform that I could include in my list of projects and start building its minimum viable product because this documentary fan bot and its social media platform documentary conversations driven could be as successful as these book driven social media and who knows maybe like Shelfari this documentary social media could be acquired by Amazon prime or any other video streaming platform like CuriosityStream.

There are millions of real fans of film documentaries like me and probably like you who will be happy and eager to be a user of such film documentaries-driven social media.

This Documentary Artificial Fan and this documentary-driven social networks are two important platforms for people who will enjoy using them and I hope that they will help build a better world.
