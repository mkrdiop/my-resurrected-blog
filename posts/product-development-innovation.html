Product Development Innovation

Diop Makhtar

·1 day ago

Sometimes your collection of product ideas can share base functionalities. This core of features is a base product the two others being specialized products. Your engineering team should focus on these features first but it is not an obvious choice because the development process of each of these features has a role to play in the decision you have to take. It also not an obvious thing to find core features when you have to manage thousands of product ideas that your team holds. Artificial Intelligence is fixing this issue with a huge base of data from past successful product development projects data and a new product development framework that transform your organization into a product-driven organization always outputting innovative products and services that the market seeks and buys.
