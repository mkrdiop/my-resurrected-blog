Patterns and sticking

Patterns are keys in business, decision making ... And in so many things that we do all day long. Sticking is this act that allows us to see a pattern. Reading someone’s blog article by article, post by post will allow you to see his writing patterns, how he thinks and most importantly there will be skill transfer between you and this blogger, authors, engineer, programmers or ..
If you keep looking at the whole colony of ants it is less likely for you to see how ants walk.
Stick and you will find this pattern that will help you achieve what you want.
