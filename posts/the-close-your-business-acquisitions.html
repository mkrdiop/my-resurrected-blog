The close your business acquisitions

A methodology for finding working startup ideas that could exit successfully

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

There many startups that got acquired and shut down because the technology they were building was going to disrupt the business model of the acquiring company. If you know that could see acquisition transaction as a source of startup ideas that you could resuscitate and execute.

(1) There is this brilliant idea about messaging that clearly makes complete sense today as the technology gained more maturity and users having more sensibility to voice applications. This was behind a really good startup acquired years ago that started it. As art is something about building the next thing on top of past things you can make this idea work again if you know it.

(2) Hotmail had a famous traction strategy for its growth the one that gets talked about often, this is this PS message at the bottom of each email but the thing that ignited Hotmail is more than that and involve graph theory and knowledge even if at that time social network was not the thing. If you know this real thing that propulsed Hotmail and if you use it with (1) it is very likely that you will manage a really successful startup with millions of users.
