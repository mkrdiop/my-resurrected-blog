This slow bus

You don’t need data to know how smartphones are used in Africa and I guess in all poor countries. You just look at them using this device. Facebook and WhatsApp that’s it. We hold this powerful device which is a computer with a small screen but all we do with is interrupting our friends and family with voices, texts and photos. Of course, interactions between persons is important and it is for sure not the best time to state that.
I saw all these scooters running on this boulevard and I cannot use my smartphone tell where I want to go for one of them to pick me and bring me to this place in exchange of maybe a bit more then this slow bus I took cost me.
Should build this scooter boy app, not for delivery but transportation of people.
