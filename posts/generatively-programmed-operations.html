Generatively Programmed Operations

When generative AI makes APPS and Systems Integration easier

GPO here doesn’t stand for Group Policy Object (GPO) and Why it is Important for system administrators, system engineers, and DevOps but it stands for getting to automated operations from simple to complex prompts what is a solution in this category of generative AI that one can call Text to Operations or Text to Tasks or Text to Actions, a category that is about automating things firstly apps, systems, and networks operations what will likely ease the job of people that manage technical stack because with this solution technical workers will less and less deal with general and domain-specific language like C/C++ and … in favor of Natural languages like English and French.

automation has always been one of the most powerful effects of computing. since mainframe computers, we have always tried to automate most of our office work and personal computers widened the opportunity for business automation in offices and factories. With computers widely adopted and the internet broadly accessible to humankind we started developing websites and software and process and display business and consumers information. as the number of software on the market and inside enterprises continued to grow the need to make these software communicate became more and more important what made us come up with concepts like Enterprise Service Bus and other protocols that allow the design and deployment of distributed systems made of pieces of technology that communicate to form complex sets of business operations.

integration is what we call this domain that is about connecting apps and software for them to exchange data and for their users and administrator to set up and run simple or complex flows of operations that require the inputs and output of many apps and software that use to be isolated in the host enterprise IT. Microsoft Biztalk was one of these solutions that was allowing enterprises to integrate their apps, software, and systems but simpler solutions like Zapier, PowerAutomate, and IFTTT emerged during the shift from desktop software to software as services (SaaS).

Integration services like Zapier, PowerAutomate, and IFFTT gluing apps, software, and systems for them to work in sync has become easier and easier but it always requires the user to set up integration flows and define parameters and sometimes call their programming skills. With the emergence of Artificial intelligence, it becomes evident that integrating apps, software, and systems will become even easier because tech stacks will become smart enough to setup and program themselves to do a given set of tasks given an event but generative AI make it even easier because with generative AI we could setup integration flows without using programming languages or complex configuration of APIs but by using natural language prompts. We could even imagine that AI will be able to spot the needed automation by processing and analyzing how we use apps, software, and systems.

when I receive mail from Y take this email and forward it to Z, once done create a new invoice in salesforce and a page on Notion, and invite all the marketing team to this notion page IS an example of a natural language prompt that one could type for this Generatively Programmed Operation Stack to create the code that is needed for this described operation to be performed.
