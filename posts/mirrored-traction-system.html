Mirrored traction system

India helped Hotmail grow in the US

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

when Hotmail entered India it grew there faster than it was growing in terms of user registration in the US and what is curious is that that’s India who helped Hotmail grow in the US because Indians found Hotmail as a great tool to speak to their friends and family who was working and living in US. of course telephone helped the travel of the message from India to Us because that’s on the phone that they were telling to those in the US that email was cheaper for them because that’s free. So those in the US started creating accounts and telling also their American Indian friends to use Hotmail. Not sure but think that the fact the Indian immigrants working in tech are many make the adoption of this new tech product faster. It is a curious traction pattern that I am describing here. You could name this traction pattern mirrored traction. With a mirrored traction you use another audience to target a particular audience you are interested in because people are more likely to accept things from their relations than for you, or for an entrepreneur, a startup, company, or whatever else.

If Hotmail had focused on the US market maybe it would never be the Hotmail we know now as a fast adopted web platform, one of the early high and fast grew startups.

I could add this as a PS field but of course, added to this effect was also the PS hack on the bottom of every message which was a clever working idea of the Hotmail’s team that is still used today.
