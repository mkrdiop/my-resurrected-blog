The world noise map

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
4 min read
·
10 hours ago

--

This sounds like a good feature for Google Maps, an ambient noise level of each street of the world and this idea is easy to implement because all that is needed is to embed a microarray on the cars that make these pictures that become google street map. If I had a smartphone I would be able to start this idea by walking around my neighborhood for recording the ambient noise and I guess my street would have been part of the world’s nosiest places. Also one could see that with satellite pictures of Google map, one could spot the level of anarchic occupation of streets and public space by commercials and professional independent workers like woodworkers, and car repairers, mainly in Africa in countries like Senegal and cities like Dakar like this

I don't know where I got this effect that seems like a disease that is this disturbing feeling of hearing noise of particular objects behaving on particular surfaces like a pencil used on a piece of white paper or chalk or marker used on a whiteboard or blackboard but some. For example, I heard steve job talking about how bad he feels when he hears the noise of a pencil on paper. I am part of those who feel bad and nervous when they are in a noisy environment with noises that are not in my control and again I don't know why. I think this started with my increasing willingness to be in a quiet place for being more inspired and better placed for thinking about what made me decide to work during nights when everybody around me is sleeping and the world becomes quiet from my point of hearing. I don't know if it's a bad or a good thing but I am sure that many people are like me. people like steve job and this brave woman rescued from this catastrophic accident of this boat of my country called le Jola which went deep inside the Atlantic ocean taking with it several men, women and sadly kids that would have been promising men and women who would have been able to push our country forward like this team of young footballers who may have been 100x more successful than a Sadio Mane or a Lionel Messy. I have heard this rescued woman telling how bad she feels when she is in a noisy environment because this reminds her of the difficult experience of hearing people locked inside the boat crying during this catastrophic boat accident what makes me think that in my situation this effect could come from one of the hard past personal situations I left by being miraculously rescued but these past situations were not so hard then being in a boat which is going to about to …..

I decided to skip whole chapters narrating these 3 past personal experiences during which I had been rescued when I was so close to death because these past personal experiences are little in front of the experience of the passengers of this boat, Le Jola I was talking about and all these other accidents of transportation means like cars, boats, and planes but what’s important is to know that there are people who once rescued from these kinds of accidents live with a strange effect that is about having a bad mood when plunged inside a noisy environment what make me things that information people about the noise level of streets and places of the world may matter to them and to every one of us.

with this world noise map, one could have the opportunity to know the quietest places in the world. Maybe it’s more opportunistic for Airbnb to implement this idea than Google but writing about it I remembered my grandparents who during the last part of their time lived near the runway of what was the most used airport in Senegal when before that they were living when I am actually living, a place I have already told you in the first paragraph of this article how noisy it is and how anarchically is its range of streets occupied making it difficult to for one to walk or drive his or her car to home. For sure it’s hard to live in such a noisy and anarchically occupied place but it’s where I grew up and from there I learned a lot when I was a kid and now that I am back here since 2012 and there are great people here like in other quieter or noisier places of the world. To say that what I have tried to explain is not related to people because there are great people everywhere no matter what is the level of noise but I think that this world noise map is a good idea and perhaps it’s a map that is available somewhere on the world wide web of in an app on android or iPhone.

if you know such kind of map please let me know. if you could implement it please start right now and let me know how I could help.
