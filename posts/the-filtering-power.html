The filtering power

Diop Makhtar

·Nov 12, 2020

The filtering power has changed camp. it used to be in the hands of deciders from majors for the music industry, publishers for the book industry, producers for the film industry, media owners for information... Each industry had people into that had decision power to tell which is good or not. The internet had taken this power and put it on to the hands of everyone who is connected. Now everybody has the power to say what is good or not. That’s the magical part of the internet this tool for democratization. This tool is achieving what all great democracies failed to do which is
Giving people the power to filter and to decide what is good for them.
