Third-Party Apps are maturing

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
2 min read
·
Dec 7, 2020

--

There is still room s for building third party application that can be used by website owners for making their visitor experience better. I may be the first to coin VX as Visitor Experience while everybody talks about UX. One that wants to build such a third-party application must be creative enough to come up with app ideas that help webmasters, bloggers, and content marketers enrich and make content smarter for Visitors. The first vague of this kind of third party application started with Visitor Interaction with commenting third-party app like Disqus or messaging app like intercom going far into an app like useproof that deal into website event live notification. Artificial intelligence will have something to do with the second vague of third party application that will modernize more again websites. I think to be in the frontline of this vague with a lot of innovative ideas to ship. Those ideas work around blogs, books, and videos. I am glad to know that we are in the connection area and those who figure out how to connects two parties are those who will win. This is so obvious for someone who believes in God because unity is what we seek and making communication between two happen to make it more easy and probable to happens. Not Christian but it is also obvious that the trinity is a clue here.

Hey, third Party Business Model Generation is a skill that you should learn. I am writing about it and hope this will one day helps you master it.
