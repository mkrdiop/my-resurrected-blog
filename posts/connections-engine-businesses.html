Connections Engine Businesses

Does your idea connect persons who seek to be connected to each other?
It happens that ideas that bring connections to people are the ones that win. Internet is a connection engine that transformed our industrial economy into a connection economy. In a connection economy, middle businesses are the most successful because they don't need a huge manufacturing plant to make connections happen. Just with some bare metal servers and even the silliest algorithm one can get a billion dollar business.
