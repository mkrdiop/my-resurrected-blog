I am very surprised by all the correspondence I am discovering between languages since I am interested in the relationships among world languages precisely my native language, English, French, and Arabic (here, here, here). Only these natural languages because there are the only ones I am comfortable with despite my willingness to learn others. Today Like I did before I discovered another cue that of languages that shows how deep our tongues are linked telling us and reminding us that we are coming from the same root. As of today, I learned a new English word which is obituary when I was working on an idea about business modeling.

Like I often do once I got an interesting idea, I went to GoDaddy in order to try to find a relevant domain name for this idea. I tried several names of the idea related to obituaries and ended with I don't know how with a two-word domain name with TAG as the first one. This kind of word that merges two words for a new meaning is called portmanteau and this is the solution of the text game I proposed to you here. As I said, once I discovered this domain name that starts with tag the word tag was sticking on my mind like for telling Hey there is something happening here with me and this idea about obituaries that you working on. It was not hard for me to see that the word TAG was phonetically close to the word TAGé (pronounce the accent like in french) which in my native language describes the action of disclosing publically the death of someone and I believe that you are smart enough to know that this action and the action of tagging are the same. When we disclose something, we are tagging it making it easy for others to see, know, and acknowledge. Languages tell us who we are, where we come from, and also how we think.
