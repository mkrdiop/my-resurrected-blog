Tribe investments grouped interest

Diop Makhtar

Just now·1 min read

We individual investors have now the market trend decision power because technology has spread and the internet so wide and fast that we now can become market markers by tribe or crowd investing massively in the company that cares for us.

Our financial future is there held in one of our hands or in one of our pockets or it is there on our desk. We used to delegate this power to banks, institutional funds, or hedge funds but now we have enough information and computing power to make smart high impact investment decision alone or by the mean of a well-informed crowd or tribe name it how you want but I mean a group of persons that have enough empathy to see that they are by no means different and who are highly capable of deciding and executing in a synchronous or asynchronous way the same thing.

Now tribe investments grouped interest matters a lot today and into the near and long foreseen future we knew it years ago and worked early on it to be the most valued and knowledge led tribe of the world.

What we will do with it must be a very good thing for everybody.
