Yes she/he can disrupt your business

There are many companies that claim to be global, international, inclusive, equal opportunity driven but still so few of them considering talent outside of their domestic markets or headquarters. Few efforts are done for discovering world talents that are based in unexpected countries. The same can be said for these angel investors who think that a young man from Africa cannot break the silicon valley ecosystem with breaking through market-driven innovation. If your CEO, c suite executive and investors don’t learn how to see you will never see this one that will disrupt your business.
