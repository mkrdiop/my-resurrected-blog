Diabetes is becoming an even more critical world healthcare concern with 422 million people affected by this disease according to the world health organization (WHO) which is expecting this number to rise to 642 million by 2040 which is an increase in the number of diabetes patients in less than two decades.

Dexcom, inc, a medical device company that develops, manufactured, and distributes glucose monitoring (CGM) systems for people with diabetes could likely profit from the increasing number of people who DexcomDexcomwill be affected by diabetes by providing them with cost-saving solutions like rtCGM because diabetes is known to have an expensive cost of treatment. for example, in 2021, the total cost of diabetes in the United States was estimated to be $327 billion.

In this article, we will explore how the evolution of the number of diabetes patients and the economics could positively impact the stock price of Dexcom thanks to possible increasing adoption of its ranges of solutions designed for patients living with both diabetes of type 1 and type 2 a well as preventive solutions that could target the 1.5 billion adults who live with prediabetes which is a condition in which blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not high enough to be diagnosed as diabetes.

We will first try to use historical data about the number of diabetes to see if year over year there is a sensibility of the yearly revenues of Dexcom to the evolution of the number of diabetes patients.

The last chart above which plots Dexcom’s revenue in function of the number of diabetes patients shows a very high positive correlation between the two.
