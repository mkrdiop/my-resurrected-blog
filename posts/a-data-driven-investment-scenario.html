a Data-driven investment scenario

This is very likely to be not compliant with regulations

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

self-match prevention trades which is now a feature of all exchanges and seem to be a new way of spoofing and it can also be a profitable strategy for an investment firm that exploits it by leveraging machine learning with enough data about past executed orders that allows having precise information about orders that are induced by other orders.

Here is an example: a market participant posts sell orders every time the limit book sell-side size is more than 1000 shares. Your algorithm cannot know the participant but the data that you gathered and your machine learning system know that every time the sell-side size is 1000 another 200 shares are added by this unknown participant. If this occurs let’s say for GME ticker and now the sell-side size is 900 at $500.01 but you want to buy right now 1100 shares at this price point, not more. Given that the behavior holds because of the likelihood of the event described is almost sure to happen. Your systemic trading algorithm sends an order to sell 100 shares of GME inducing the unknown participant to post 200 shares making the sell-side size becomes 1300 shares. Once the order of the unknown participant is posted your system HFT algorithm automatically sends an 1100 buy order with a self matching prevention. What will happens is that even if your sell order is in front of the 200 shares of the unknown market participant it will not be executed but canceled. What will be executed are the 900 shares that were there and the 200 shares that came after your …. order. The whole will make 1100 shares.

This is an exploitation scenario that may not be compliant (surely not) with the regulation by exploiting a feature of an Exchange and requires a fast trading system surely Exchange colocated and smart data-driven trading algorithms. But this very difficult for regulators to track it and I think Exchanges also.

PS: GME is here just used as an example of a sticker but has nothing to do with this data-driven investment scenario.
