The framing effect and product presentation

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Presenting a product feature or an idea in order to make visitors take the action you want them to take is not an obvious thing to do. It requires knowledge about marketing and you will see that cognitive science plays also a big role. What cognitive science does with selling products or ideas online?

As your goal is to make visitors take action you should at least know basic ok knowledge about how the brain works. For that, you have a lot of resources to start with like reading scientists like Lakoff…. One of the basic concepts of cognitive science is the framing effect that defines how emotions can be modified by the way information is presented, framed. This framing effect does such that you are more reassured if you are told that 90% of patients survive after a given surgery than if you were told that 10% die. The negative or positive side of the information or the story plays an important role in the emotions activated. If you want your audience to feel the sentiment of urgency you should tell the story taking the negatives side even if in proportion the positive side is way bigger. For example, for selling SEO service to companies and website owners a platform makes an analysis of a website and tells how fast the web pages of the website are? The SEO agency designed its interface naively showing the information like this.
