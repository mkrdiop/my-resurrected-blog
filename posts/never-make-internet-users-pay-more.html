Never make internet users pay more

Diop Makhtar

·Just now

Resizing your images according to the device of the internet users may not make sense for you but know that internet users pay by volume of data consumed. If you take that into it will never make sense to ship a 1-gigabit image where a 1bit image is needed. And please consider the time spent by the browser to get this 1-gigabit image. there are plenty of ways of getting things done but let’s try to take the best way. Update your images before Google updates its ranking algorithm. This world Wide Web can be cleaner by our effort please also remember that we are trying to make it clean with a project that seeks to fix 404 links. How many times did you land on a 404 page?
