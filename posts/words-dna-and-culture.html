Words, DNA, and culture

Like DNA of bacteria, viruses cells and human beings mutate our language also does since we got the usage of voice. In fact, DNAs are chemical blocks of worlds that translate to biological and psychological characteristics. If we take the whole set of words of a language like English since its emergence we get the DNA of this language as all human beings share the same root, taking the DNA, I mean the set of words or dictionary, of another language we can see how these languages mutated to be the ones they are now phonetically and graphically. Is it hazardous that UP in English refers to concepts associated with height and this African language that I speak tells TUP to say jump and please notice how jump mutated to keep the radical UP? A natural Language, be it natural or computerized like python, c++, javascript or java, teaches us a lot about our history and brain. It is a cultural tool shaped by us and the environment we live in.
