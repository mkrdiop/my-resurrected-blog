Around You technology

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
Dec 2, 2020

--

Around you is this beautiful piece of technology that notifies you of everything happening around you that may interest you. Right there on all your devices, computers, smartphone and smart TV around you is all around you covering all topics that matter.

Around you technology should be built to empower people. To give them the power to do great things. To achieve their personal goals. Around you technology so should be managed by great persons. Those who don’t and will never put themselves more important than others but those who believe that they have the power to make everyone and everything better.
