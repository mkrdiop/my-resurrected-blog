Marketing to desperate startup founders and job hunters

Emotional Marketing Thoughts from the rejection letters of A16Z and Microsoft

on January 24, 2024, during the night, I received this 550FCFA phone’s credit from this friend and converted the 500FCFA to an internet pass and jumped on my computer eager to get to my mailbox to see if I got good news from a job application or from a16z with its crypto startup school. unfortunately, I got one rejection letter from Microsoft and an email from a16z telling me that I was not selected for its crypto startup school.

I was pissed off twice but what surprised me was that the last paragraph of the rejection letter from a16z had three links that lead to their materials like podcast, youtube channel, and newsletter. Once I saw that I said to myself that businesses aren’t ashamed of promoting and advertising their services and products on such kinds of emails that hold bad news for the recipients even if this email from a16z I am referring to was holding links to materials that are useful to candidates. I thought that this is not emotionally a good practice for a company of the size and image of a16z and even for a small-sized and less reputable company to include in communication that will negatively affect the emotion of recipients links that may provide them benefits in terms of awareness and traction like getting some visits and views of branded materials.

I looked at the email again, read it again, and recalled that a16z reported to have received 8000 applications for this crypto startup school which led me to think again about all the effort required to process all these applications but I had at the same time this suspicious thought that was telling or perhaps reminding me that these kinds of even are whatever on can say a very powerful marketing tool about traction and brand awareness for companies like a16z because I said to myself that if 8000 teams and/or individual applied then there are thousands of them who clicked on those three links and consumed branded material while being trying to fade the emotion that comes from a piece of bad news they were expected to come as good what again feels like a very powerful traction and lead generation process that doesn’t care about the actual state of emotion of the target audience.

We all know that Events are marketing tools, that’s a basic knowledge taught at business schools so there isn’t a matter to see a company exploit this marketing channel and I am not sure nor saying that a16z organized this crypto school for marketing purposes. As I know a16z, I don’t think that this venture firm needs such kind of event-driven marketing.
$28.2 billion (as of April 5, 2022) of assets under management, number 10 on the 2011 Forbes Midas List of Tech’s Top Investors, several successful startups on its portfolio with many of them hitting billion of dollars of market cap I think that these are some facts that may tend toward giving the benefit of the doubt to a16z for that it has organized this crypto startups schools for helping entrepreneurs and not for getting awareness, clicks, and views from 8000 solo entrepreneurs like me and teams and for spotting trends of ideas in the crypto space. It could just be that I am a very cynical person who sees bad things everywhere, a bad science man one could say but these kinds of links in this context of a rejection letter form a marketing and branding mistake for a company of any size that thinks carefully about the gut and sentiment of those it tries to serve.

Putting links that may lead to traction opportunities and market insights and even business opportunities in any medium that reach an audience with a piece of information that holds a piece of news that may be seen by them as bad is not a best practice — give a link to another kind of opportunity that may or may not lead to the outcome that your target was expecting from you. For the case of a16z, this could have been a link to another crypto school program of this kind with a still open application process but it is often seen as not beneficial to credit or link to competitors when most of the time this is a very powerful generous act that generates trust because people will know that you are here to help but who can imagine Google telling you to go to Bing because the term you searched for get a better result on Bing. As I have talked about Bing let me introduce you to the insight I got from the Microsoft rejection letter compared to the one from a16z.

The rejection letter I got from Microsoft contains only one link leading to the profile of the desperate jobs hunter who could repeat the process of applying to another job or track the state of the ongoing applications. In this situation, Microsoft is applying a best practice because Microsoft was able to put here a link about its last products and services for developers or something about azure but they didn’t as putting only one link that lead to its jobs board portal is a pretty good signal as if it was telling to the recipients the link is there click and go find another job offer and apply to it what makes it more likely to get a job at Microsoft. I don’t know if this is a carefully thought strategy but while writing this I thought that Microsoft would have been more trustworthy if, at the same time, it also provided a link to a page of another job board listing the same kind of jobs offer that the applicants applied for.

I am not promoting myself to Microsoft nor throwing stones at A16z, these are U.S companies that I really care about. what I am doing is only sharing thoughts, opinions, and ideas as I did with all these 700 and more blog entries almost all about the tech industry. I may not be right with everything I advanced in the paragraphs above but I could tell you that every bad news often comes with a good one if one gets the luck and bravery to consume the emotion that comes from the bad news and look carefully. This is what happened to me with the rejection from a16z because they provided me a validation signal for this micro SaaS idea I heard from Tk Kader or MicroConf ( don’t remember who) which I think is a pretty good one. I don’t know if I will be building this idea that may be useful to venture firms like A16Z that may be users of it because up until there I am like a traditionist of religion collecting interesting prophetic sayings but unlike the traditionist of religions, I could expand these ideas I get from experts and professionals sayings with my own opinions, ideas, and vision of the world and generate from them new ideas that might not work but since there most of the time I see early where things are heading in tech and the ideas that will shape the next months and years and this very earlier. For that, I am happy for how early I got to see the short- and long-term trends of tech ideas but I also think and believe like Marc Andreessen that it’s time to build but I could add that it’s always time.
