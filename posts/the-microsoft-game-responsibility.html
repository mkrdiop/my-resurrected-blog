The Microsoft game responsibility

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

Game developers, artists, and designers are passionate about technology and gaming. They are very creative, skilled, and hard-working but they often do all of this at the cost of marketing better the games they make because marketing and business administration is not something they care most about. This leaves them at the hands of some hustling marketers bargaining with their creative works using their MBA skills. It is time for the actor of the gaming industry to care also about Business Administration and marketing because Gaming has become an industry due to the revolution of personal computers that made PC become a gaming medium while improving the performance of consoles. Several game studios have been spawned and a lot of games hits build making the size of the audience of gamers bigger.

There is still room for growing the size of this audience of gamers because a lot of people like me are very willing to have more time and incentive for restarting to game and more and more are willing to start even if they are old. Then empowering game studios is important and this will be easier if all participants of the industry from platforms owners, console makers to game studio joint force to produce ever more creative and popular gaming experiences. Big tech companies like Microsoft have a big role to play for this to happen and I would agree that since the first version of Windows that’s what Microsoft is trying with success. The list of gaming technology and studios that this company integrated into its corporate portfolio can prove it without saying anything about the sustaining innovation made for the Xbox to be more and more performant.

A major shift and expansion of the gaming industry are what we should be seeking and I believe that this is more likely to happen if we leverage the power of being in the community and showing strongly that people like us do things like games. I was willing to say people like us do things like this which is the mantra of being and living in a community.

I never lost sight of the game industry since I early played games on PC and with a sega Master just after. Games are like that. once we like them we could not live without we just try to ignore them because of constraints and time. Then our core goal as actors of the game industry is to break these constraints and free time for people to play because playing can contribute to learning which in turn contributes to productivity that leads to growth. If we achieve this we will be growing with happiness.

I hope we will and I am trying my best to be.
