AI and airports graph

Diop Makhtar

Just now·2 min read

You have the flight data of this area, let say the continents like Europe or America at a given time. How the closing of one or more airports will affect global traffic? How the remaining airports will handle this airport failure by digesting the traffic that would normally be handled by the failed airports? This is an exercice about resilience in graph and the world need for sure to be prepared for such kinds of event. It happens that you can have this dataset and you can apply your machine meat ing and artificial intelligence skills to train a model that can give us clever insights about this eventually. Datasets are there for you to play with it by building predictive models and creating innovative datasets that can be features that will increase the accuracy of the models already built. Maybe like the airplanes that will be rerouted to land in an emergency in other airports than the ones that were planned you will land your dream job with this data science exercise. What if the goal was to build a resilient telecom network with these airplanes? That's what Widrone was all about, a network that connects Europe to America, Europe to Africa, Africa to Asia, Asia to Europe all with planes that embed a telecom system for people on land. Hope you will land your dream job with this.
