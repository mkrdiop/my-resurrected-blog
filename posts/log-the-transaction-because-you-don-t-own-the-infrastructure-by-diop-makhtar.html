Log the transaction because you don’t own the infrastructure.

Microservices are now king when it is about building a web system. Many of applications we are using day by day are made of more than thousands of microservices talking to each other . These system are almost all hosted in a cloud environment operated by different companies like Microsoft, Amazon, Google or the younger digital ocean. These companies help us to not deal with hardware infrastructure but when our system is made of thousands of micro services we should know where these micro services are and how much time they take for communicating. To say short we must be aware about latency. This word latency follows me so long time that I am thinking of it isn’t the most important factor in now economical era. I was dealing with it in financial market systems and when I work in tech stack it is still here. Keep your latency on track when you host you platform into the cloud. I expect latency hedging coming into tech driven businesses like it is done on financial market. I got good solutions about this issue. Do you?
