AI in Product Engineering

Diop Makhtar

10 hours ago·1 min read

Sometimes your collection of product ideas can share base functionalities or call them features if you want. This base product is the standard while the two others are specialized products. Your engineering team should focus on these standard base features first but it is not an obvious choice because the development process of each of these features has a role to play in the decision you have to take. It is also not an obvious thing to find core features when you have to manage with thousands of product ideas that your team holds. Artificial Intelligence is fixing this issue with a huge base of data from past successful product development processes and a new product development framework that transform your organization into a product-driven organization always outputting innovative products and services that the market seeks and buy.
