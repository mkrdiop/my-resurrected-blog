Our crafty hands have caused several unexpected technical damages due to our tech curiosity.

Sure, We, TechEnthousiats, have broken many of our friends and family’s devices because we were willing to learn how they works. fortunately, now with the easiness of access to information the “how it works” is available everywhere in digital format so tech enthusiasts like me and probably like you don't need anymore to open and decompile hardware and software for learning how they work. Simple proven-to-work solutions that help achieve primitive mechanical and digital actions get printed on our minds and we started assembling these primitive solutions for creatively creating other hardware and software that would allow us to do more with more efficiency and less effort, to understand better the world around us and to better connect with others about whom we care about while hoping to allocate the extra time saved from the use this connectivity and productivity based technical solutions to things we think worth concentring our effort for achieving more than what we want to achieve but few technical solutions around really ends helping us safe time. But it is sad to see that many of them reduce our attention span and make us less concentrated on things that matter most and this without leaving your pocket safe from cash leakage give if unlike me you have some amount of cash to spend on products like these technical solutions which all claim to procure connectivity and productivity.

connectivity and productivity are two powerful materials like these nuclear fissile materials I have been writing about in my last article. Like in the collision of a neutron with an atom of uranium or plutonium, the collision of connectivity and productivity creates an energy that put people and organizations forward. what’s hard to attain is to get to this chain reaction that increases the number of collisions between what we could call the atoms of productivity and the atoms of connectivity. The understanding of how to collide productivity and connectivity for producing more energy for self and organization positive motion is, in my opinion, a key lever for those who want to lead and create world-impacting things like these technical products I was been talking about since the beginning of this article but you could guess that this goes far beyond these technical products only.

who is good at managing the nuclear reaction chains of atoms of productivity and atoms of connectivity =, be it a system, an organizational unit or a simple human being like you and me is for sure a leader the world needs because these reaction chains like the ones that happen every day in nuclear power plants are what power our actual economy and this is expected to be more emphasized in near and far future as technology will continue to be easier, more accessible and I hope also more interoperable because connectivity is also about easing communication between systems of whatever interface and core.
