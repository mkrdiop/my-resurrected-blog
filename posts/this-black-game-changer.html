I was drafting another game idea (The World War of Brands) while I said to myself that I should write about this black African American genius for claiming my recognition of his work as I used to play with cartridge with my sega Console years ago when I was Kid. I still have a picture of me playing with this console and just yesterday was talking about Moon Walker this game that featured Michael Jackson another black genius that shaped the world with dance and music. I will for sure make a rant about Mark E. Dean this other black man that revolutionized the computer world with the ISA bus. And remember that Isa bus allowed computers to go faster than this bus.
