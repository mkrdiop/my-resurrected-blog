Questions about eReaders

Diop Makhtar

1 day ago·2 min read

What if the ebook eReader comes with 1000 titles of different genres? What if sharing is the only way to read them? the reader shares the ebook before she or he can read the offered ebook. What if we find a new way to make the cover visible to the people around the reader? What if this cover can be the cover of an ebook different than the one the reader is reading? so many questions about the reading experience but what about the publishing experience? How can publishers get data that can allow them to publish better books? what about data that allow them to learn how ebook spread?

is there an opportunity to offer a Reader better than the ones that are offered in today's ebook market?

My answer is yes but seeing an opportunity and deploying all effort and strategies to exploit are two different things. Marketing an ebook Reader Device where there are more than three alternatives is a real marketing challenge that could be addressed only with deep knowledge about reading behaviors, publishing market analysis, and clear and linchpin positioning that puts the readers and the publisher in the center of the problem by trying to achieve this two things :

Make reading experience (RX) better

Make Publishing Experience (PX) better

Everything other than these two things is secondary.
