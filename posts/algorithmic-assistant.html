Algorithmic assistant

Diop Makhtar

·Feb 9

This book you are reading and those already consumed.

These websites you have visited today and last days.

These blog articles read all along.

All these pieces are words and actions that this smart algorithm can take as inputs and find the relationships they have with your actual actions, plan, thoughts, projects, and writings, and of course, it could output for you the list of prioritized future opportunities. This opportunity you have with Google start applying in May 2021 it could tell you right now.

This algorithmic assistant has medical skills that could ease the life of so many patients like me. Why would think that algorithms could not help reanimate a patient who has a psychological disaster from an accident or a surgery or just by the story of life?
