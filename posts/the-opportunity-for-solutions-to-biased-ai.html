The opportunity for Solutions to Biased AI

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
4 min read
·
Just now

--

I have heard it again from the mouth of Cedric Villani the French mathematician talking with journalists from Libération that is this danger of AI amplifying bias like favoring male profiles over female profiles when asked a list of CEOs or amplifying the negative judgment of a legal case presented to it because of the color of the skin of the concerned person. This problem of biased AI is just an extension of this same problem that we have seen with search and recommendation engines like in the example given by Cedric Villani who reported a study he made with his student by quantifying the number of men and women provided by a search engine when the keyword CEO was entered. This is an interesting study that one could try to expand to job boards by searching for profiles of software engineers on these job boards and assessing how many female and male profiles will be in the result provided by the job board’s results and comparing these numbers with public census data.

The problem of AI bias is very concerning because we tend to enter a world where actions and decisions will derive more and more from smart AI agents which will gonna tell who is right and who is wrong (judicial cases ) or who will get the job and who will not (HR) or who will be funded and who will not (Venture Capital). As any kind of problem is an opportunity for searching for solutions and entrepreneurs are those who try to find solutions to problems in order to get financial advantage from them. I think that while many entrepreneurs working hard to design and develop smart generative AI it may be opportunistic for some others to try to design and develop tools and AI that spot and fix biases of the actual and coming AI solutions like chatGPT, and Bard.

Building such kind of AI bias fixer will require a deep knowledge of topics sociology approached with a statistical and sociological mindset like what have pioneered by sociologists like Pierre Bourdieu and his students and peers and I am among those who think that the huge amount of data about human interactions that we have collected through social media platforms like Twitter, Facebook, Instagram should be used by cautious hands and minds for the goal of developing these AI systems that smart at fixing biased AI. I am not sure that these social media platforms used by billion of people around the world are actually working on such kind of solution but I think they should. Maybe I am not well informed about these social media future projects but what’s sure is the world needs AI systems that are built for fighting all forms of biases and discrimination.

As you can see there are so many overwhelming things to do with AI that one could be busy spending time spotting the number of opportunities what I then advise to you if you are trying to contribute in this field of AI is to pick an opportunity and to work on it and I think that the more you will narrow the size of the problem and audience of the AI system you are interested to work on the more you increase your chance of success. This is something taught by the Minimum viable product and the minimum audience methodologies promoted by professionals and business thinkers like Seth Godin and steve blank. For example, I have been working and writing about what I had called SwapJS something that many of you found interesting if I judge by the number of people who read the article about it and commented but these ideas have been now narrowed to a very tiny problem impacting a smaller audience of structural engineers and/or industrial instrumentation engineers. I can’t tell you much about this new version of SwapJS because I just started to work on it but I think that like me you could look at the problem of Biased AI and try to find small problems related to it and try to build an AI solution that fixes it. for example, you could develop an AI that helps job boards and HR consultants avoid biased decisions in front of a list of candidates.

Again AI is overwhelmingly taking a portion of our day-to-day work and life and you could do something interesting with it whether you are an entrepreneur or a simple user. Then go play with all these AI around you and find how you could contribute with your entrepreneurial skills and how they could help you get your job done faster, easier, smarter, and better but more importantly who they could have a positive impact on people and organizations around you because the world is a whole and remember that the world is not only the world wide web then there maybe interesting things to do with AI in things far from the web. Think about this and thank you for reading.
