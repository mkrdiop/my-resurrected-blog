Are timed investments better?

The stock options business

Diop Makhtar

Just now·3 min read

Timed investment is an investment that is bounded to a time period meaning that there is a clear and fixed exit date.

Options and futures are these kinds of investments. These types of timed investments make the investor more focused and able to make more investment bets while making the entrepreneur more aware of the importance of time.

Holding stocks can lead to boredness and we are exposed to a frequency of trends reversals that could emotionally affect us if it takes too much time while with timed investments we know that whatever will happen to the stock or investment vehicle it will end a defined time spot that we could not change. Without this time constraint, an investor could keep delaying the exit because she finds herself in unfavorable positions letting herself by the way at the mercy of the financial system into which she would benefit from. I think we are more capable of managing things that don't have an impact on our decision process due to their behaviors.

If you are a data-driven investor I believe that you should focus on timed investments like options and forwards and this is more true if you are an algorithmic data-driven investor. And also know that the number of times that you will try impact always your level of success because the more you will give a try the more you have a chance to win. That’s the rewarding part of timed investments, it is time-constrained and as time is the most scarce resource, it is better to know how much of it we will spend on everything that we try because it allows us to know how many times we could try again. If you are trading options that expire every month you know that you can try 12 times per year. you have more luck to win trying to invest 12 times in Microsoft in a year than investing 1 time into it.

These are facts are among others that made me decided to focus on options investing and adding options trading features to the stock trading engine I have built in the past “Oui Predict” and sincerely options investment connected me to high skilled traders and data scientists and really fueled my view of business models and operations because for of these time is an important resource to know about how to manage it better for achieving your goal.

If you are a data-driven investor then options trading is something worth trying but please learn a lot before making huge investments and if you have the ability to make it algorithmic do it because options trading is more about computing math greeks than it is about gambling with dates and prices. There are sources of options data that you can find in platforms like Quantconnect and I highly recommended the SeekingAlpha team to add features about options contents and data to its amazing platform that I use a lot and into Which I am planning to write articles about company valuation as I promised months earlier.

Startup angel VCs have not still in my sense discovered a means to help entrepreneurs with timed investment because they invest a lot of money in one company that they believe in, money that could be split into more timed investments in several ventures making her making his chance of return more probable and us more luck to get a life and world-changing tech-enabled services or product from a VC funded startups.

While writing this article I saw that there is room for entrepreneurship in timed investments like options for you data-driven investors who want also to become entrepreneurs. It’s an idea of venturing and idea into the fintech space and I will certainly write about it in the coming days as I did for many tech-enabled ideas I had. This tech-enabled fintech idea will target a small portion of the investments market by applying the theory of the innovator dilemma and the practice of the minimum viable audience.
