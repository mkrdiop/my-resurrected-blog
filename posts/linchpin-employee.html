Linchpin employee

An employee who brings value to the enterprise he or she is working for is a linchpin employee. Seem the industrial complex made all it can to not allow employee know exactly how much value they bring to a company. If this was done without the sake of turning employees to compete each against each others this would lead us to a better economy, linchpin economy of linchpin services and products.
