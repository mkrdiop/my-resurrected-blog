Product Design Story

Product design is not about bringing something new. The best products are often old ideas that are redesigned to tell a different story than what they used to tell. Product design like marketing is about storytelling. By choosing a given shape or colour the designer should be sure that this shape or colour helps make the story resonates with the target audience of the product.
If you want tto be a good product designer and marketer learn storytelling.
