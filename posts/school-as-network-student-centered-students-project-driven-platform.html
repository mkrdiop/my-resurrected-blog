School as Network, student-centered students project-driven platform

I designed and developed this project of student-centered and project-driven platform named synergizr in 2014.

I was Conviced that education should be more engaging for students and should be using more online medium driven while being open to any kind of partners willing to participate in. I am still more and this pandemic gives me more reason to do. Education is simply a network that connect people with same interest and ignite and facilitate their interactions such that they will be able to discover and do more about what they are interested in. It is not about teachers, professors, mentors... and students. Not any more. It’s about ideas and people that interact to output interesting and useful things. These things can be anything like products, technology, services art ... Like I was listing the field of today’s universities. In fact we give our faculties the names of what we seek to produce with. With the faculty of medicine we expect better medicines not good doctors that can work cheaper and cheaper for the benefit of the industrial complex. Of course we need very good doctors but what we really need is better medicines and better persons to apply these medicines better.

School is simply a network
