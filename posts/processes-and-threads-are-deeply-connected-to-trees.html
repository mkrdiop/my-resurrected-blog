Processes and threads are deeply connected to trees

How can a developer make better and faster systems without knowing something about trees and processes? Every single language use trees for translating syntactic code into machine code. I was just interested in coding things until I ended caring about making them better and faster systems for that I needed to know deeper how the wheel turns. I started Crunching world best programming language engines to know how they can be made better. It was not an easy task to do but I thought it was something worth doing as systems are becoming more and more complex and connected. Often a standalone system is better than a system that does everything. I found the name of my next venture today. It is …….one.com — still don't have the $100 for the domain.

I wrote this post after seeing the ad of the master class of Dan Brown into which he says write bad things until you write a very good novel. I heard this statement so many times from the mouth of him.
