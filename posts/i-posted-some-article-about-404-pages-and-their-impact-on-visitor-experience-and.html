Cleaning the internet

I posted some article about 404 pages and their impact on visitor Experience and energy waste. I thing we should do our best to clean them out of the world wide web. Why not a 404 search engine and fixer? That the question I asked myself and I just figure out the best answer was to build one and to reward those websites who do their best for deleting 404 links on their websites. I have no revenue plan for this just the sake of having a cleaner and energy friendly internet. Will soon make this engine that can be seen as vacuum cleaner for the internet. Hope you will support with your effort because cleaning the www matters a lot.
