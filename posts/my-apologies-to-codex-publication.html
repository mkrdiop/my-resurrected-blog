and the 4 Fields of focus of everyone who codes

Just now·2 min read

I Just came to see that months from now I have not published on CodeX this good publication like those in which I was publishing because this publication was just there hidden into the menu for submitting articles. By accidentally landing on this article about AI in Video Publishing platform which was published in CodeX I remarked that since then I had never published something in this publication. Publishing in publications other than CodeX has been and is still an opportunity for me because it helps me to get people like you reads the ideas that I am willing to share but I should right now assume and agree that I lost a lot by not publishing in Codex because getting people like you to read my ideas is for sure worth an uncomputable value because I know the importance of people’s attention. That’s why I am trying hard to evaluate businesses using an attention-driven business valuation method.

This method will need coding pieces of code for gathering data and implementing smart algorithms for data analysis and statistical modeling to not say data science. Then this could be seen as a work about AI in business valuation and a useful tool for business investors and professionals mostly those who care about business in tech. I hope that women in tech will engage themselves in this and profit a lot from it equally with the men in tech.

All this week was about the gaming industry and will be like that because of these interesting games experiences and tech ideas that I have written about here and other articles but Gaming in an industry then it is important to know about Business administration and modeling. I am glad that I give to it this importance and I would like to see you do the same because getting more revenues will help you produce more important works and do more for fun and for learning.

Business Administration and Mathematics are the best fields for understanding and manipulating quantities, variations, and the relationships ( Link ) between objects that Computer Science can take, digitize and simulate. How all of this could go without Finance at All.

Focusing on These 4 Fields and Learning them with a problem-based approach is something that matters for everyone who CODES.
