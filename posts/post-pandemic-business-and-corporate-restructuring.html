Post pandemic Business and Corporate restructuring is in progress

Several industries are going to restructure their business and corporate model because this pandemic era highlighted some change to make the system ready for a period like this one we experienced. This period has shown us that we can do even more than we were doing with less energy and movement. We just need to optimize it for people to not feel like working is a hard and boring experience but something for them to be happy and have a better life experience closer to their families, friends, and persons that care for them.

This is possible with great ideas driven by this technology we have built. We tested during this period let’s assess the data we have got and improve what should be improved and fix what should be repaired.

Data shows us already that business travel will be reshaped and airlines contrarily than what one could think will get a lot benefit from this pandemic experience because curiously people will be more willing to travel around the world my point is that it is the safer behavior we should have given that we have deployed all these health tracking systems we have built. For each sector of all industries, we have insights about what’s bad, good, or better. Good is something we can achieve with technology but BETTER is only achievable with PEOPLE.
