Revenue stream & innovation dilemma

Trying to capture the revenue stream share of a big company. This revenue stream share is 7% of the total revenue of this company. Given the innovation dilemma concept, knowing the revenue stream structure of a big company is a great source of knowledge because we could with that have the revenue stream share that contributes less to the profitability of this company. Disruptors start with this revenue stream share with a minimal simply designed and working product and a great marketing core that let them win and extend to the next bigger revenue stream until they completely disrupt this company.
