This Internet of Real Persons before this Internet of Things

Diop Papa Makhtar

·
4 min read
·
Just now

--

what to write about when you have no idea and when you have committed to write and publish every day at least 500 words for others to read. this is what’s actually happening to me while I am writing these words you are reading to tell you that I don't know where I am heading with these words that you are reading. Maybe this article will end by talking about the business of technology like what most of my articles often do or it will become a motivational article for those who are looking for positive signals that could help them churn the actual negative flow they are experiencing

perhaps I could write about the globalization of our world because that’s somehow this globalization that led people like me to get the chance to write for people like you to read when we are separated by thousands of miles between us that have been shrunk by the digitization of this globalized world we are living in. Being in a small African country and seeing people around the world engage with what you are doing is fulfilling even if it’s a dozen of persons like you who do engage with it and this provides a bit more energy to do the next thing that could be writing another article but time is elapsing in a space that seems continuously increasing, what’s terrifying when the outcome we get from our actions keep undervalued in our point of view. then how to do impactful self-satisfying things that have value in our point of view is a question worth asking because the satisfaction we get from the results of our past actions helps us start the next one with a bit more confidence what makes it more likely for us to achieve the goal attached with these next things we set out to do.

you could see in the two paragraphs above how I diverted from the business of tech which was my core topic of writing and how I started writing about what makes us humans that is about emotions and feeling because before computers and automated machines, there are heart beating creatures on the world who are worth taking attention to and this is truer when everything related to technology becomes easier and easier while everything related to living creatures like human become more and more complex to deal with and to understand. If we want to build a better world it may be useful to put computers and machines apart and to take the time to listen, watch, observe, learn, and engage with living bodies because understanding the algorithms of mother nature is way more important than mastering the algorithms machines and computers which can easily be generated by a generative AI.

But how to listen, watch, observe, learn, and engage with living bodies when being alone in a room with a connected computer when the internet that was about to ease this has become a machine for selling products, services, and ideas rather than connecting real living bodies like you and me for us to know more about us, about our humanity and about our shared roots.

With the preceding paragraph, we have a good way for this article that you reading to transition from humanity matter to tech business matter because from this paragraph you sense this need of designing and building more human tech products like a more human internet of real persons highly willing to connect and do things together. Before we get to this Internet of Things that will be the core of the mixed reality world that is coming, I think it’s important for us to build this Internet of real persons that is mainly about easing and scaling human connections because if we look closer we could see that our world has been more put forward by connected real persons than by machines, computers, and algorithms. if we were to estimate the impact of each part of what we call technology on the world's progress we would easily see that part of it that ease and scale connections not only among human but also among systems are those who contribute most to this progress. That’s why I care about writing and sharing on blogging platforms like Medium and social media like Twitter because if they are well-operated these are part of these pieces of technology that help people listen, watch, observe, learn, and engage with living bodies. I hope they do because we really need this internet of real persons that connects real persons and help them achieve together what they seek to achieve.
